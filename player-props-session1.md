<div class="prop-card">
<div class="prop-card-label">Sealed Note — found on a Restorer zealot</div>

*A single sheet of paper, rolled and sealed with dark red wax. The seal is a circle with a small sun-ring pressed into the centre. The paper inside is dry, precise — written with the care of someone who knows this document will be read by strangers.*

---

> The willing heart gives freely what the bound heart cannot.
>
> You were hired to investigate. You will find the truth.
> When you do — ask yourself who benefits from your finding it,
> and whether those people were willing.
>
> This is not a threat. It is the only question that matters.

*No signature. The handwriting is formal, seminary-trained script.*

</div>

<div class="prop-card">
<div class="prop-card-label">Archive Door Riddle — carved into the restricted stacks door</div>

*The restricted door of the Varenhold Civic Archive, third floor. Stone, solid, cold. Carved into its face: rows of small symbols in an unfamiliar cipher. Directly below, in plain script added by a different hand at a later date:*

> *"In case the cipher proves too much — the question is plain enough once you see it."*

*Below that, in plain language:*

---

> I am given only when no one compels it.
>
> Forced, I become something else entirely.
>
> I am the difference between a gift and a theft.
>
> What word names the thing that makes the difference?

</div>

<div class="prop-card">
<div class="prop-card-label">Corven's Public Notes — shelf 4-17-3, restricted stacks</div>

*Several pages of formal notation, bound with dark cord. The cover page bears the Archive's filing stamp:*

**"Ritual of Eternal Dawn — Technical Documentation — Archmagister A. Corven."**
*Filed Year 48.*

---

**RITUAL OF ETERNAL DAWN — TECHNICAL SUMMARY**
*Archmagister A. Corven — Filed for Archive Record*

The mechanism employed in the Year 48 ritual attempt draws on distributed sympathetic resonance — a configuration in which the anchor points for the ritual's energy are distributed across multiple sites rather than concentrated at a central point.

This approach was chosen specifically because concentrated application at a single anchor risks cascade failure. The distributed model is more stable under load, though it requires precise calibration across all points simultaneously.

The living conduit mechanism was selected as the most reliable method for maintaining the distributed sympathetic anchors over time. Theoretical alternatives were considered and rejected.

**The ritual did not fail in the manner first reported.**

Further details are encoded in the supplementary notation. The key to that notation is provided separately, in three portions.

*[The remaining pages are dense notation — unreadable without the Notation Key.]*

<div class="prop-card">
<div class="prop-card-label">Restorer Pamphlet — distributed near the Dawnhall</div>

**ON THE NATURE OF THE LIGHT WE HAVE BORROWED**

*A Philosophical Address from the Society for Ritual Restoration*

---

The sun did not leave us by accident.

It was held — anchored against its nature, bound by mechanisms our predecessors understood imperfectly and executed without the consent of those most affected.

We do not blame the architects of Year 48. They acted in fear, in haste, for reasons that seemed sufficient at the time. We do not seek punishment. We seek resolution.

The amber light sustains us. We acknowledge this. But sustainability built on a foundation that was never chosen freely is not a gift. It is a debt. And debts, left unpaid, accumulate.

We are not Restorers because we wish destruction. We are Restorers because we understand that some things cannot hold forever — and it is more merciful to choose the moment than to wait until the moment chooses for us.

If you have questions, Jaret will answer them honestly.

*— The Society for Ritual Restoration, Year 98*

</div>

<div class="prop-card">
<div class="prop-card-label">Restorer Sun-Disk Token — found inside a cultist's coat</div>

*A small bronze disk, roughly the size of a coin but twice the thickness. Not currency — the face is too deliberate for that. One side bears a ring of rays pressed into the metal: a stylised sun, the Auris symbol common to all Restorer materials. The other side has a number stamped into it: **14**. Below the number, very faint, a second mark: a notch filed into the edge.*

*The disk is worn smooth at the edges from being handled often. Someone has carried this a long time.*

---

> **One side:** Sun-ring (Restorer emblem — the same symbol on their pamphlets and the sealed note's wax).
>
> **Other side:** The number 14. No explanation. No name.
>
> **Edge:** One small notch, filed deliberately, at the three-o'clock position.

*You don't know what the number means. The notch might be nothing. It might be how they know each other in a crowd.*

</div>

---

<div class="prop-card">
<div class="prop-card-label">Jaret's Dispatch — found in the note-carrier's second pouch</div>

*A folded square of paper, sealed with a plain wax thumbprint — no symbol, no ring, just a print. Inside: a short operational note in a cramped, fast hand. Not the precise liturgical script of the sealed note. Someone writing quickly, for someone they trust.*

---

> **Field cell — Lowmark, morning of the 14th.**
>
> The investigators leave the Chancellor's building at the eighth hour. You know the route. Give the warning. If they don't stop — you know what to do with the paper.
>
> Brother E. wants them rattled, not stopped. That's his call to make.
>
> Mine is different.
>
> If the paper burns, we lose nothing we didn't already know they'd find eventually. If it doesn't — that's on him.
>
> The archers hold unless I signal. They won't signal. Don't wait for it.
>
> *— J*

*No surname. No title. The initial only.*

*The handwriting is not the same as the sealed note. Two different people. Two different ideas about what this morning was supposed to accomplish.*

</div>
