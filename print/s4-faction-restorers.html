THE RESTORERS
A Society for the Return of Auris's Light
Public Pamphlet - Forty-Ninth Year of the Twilight
WE HAVE STUDIED THE FAILURE.
Not to condemn those who attempted it - may Auris receive Corven's soul, and may he be forgiven the gap between his intention and his preparation. We condemn nothing. We have learned everything we can.
We know where the error was. We know the alignment that was missing, the devotion that was insufficient, and the precise moment where what should have been a dawn became something else entirely.
We know how to do it correctly. We have always known there is a cost. We are not afraid of the cost. We are afraid of paying it for nothing - of letting the city die slowly while the answer sits undiscovered, waiting to be asked for.
FIFTY YEARS.
Every prayer answered with silence. But silence is not refusal. Silence is Auris waiting for us to be ready.
The dawn does not come to those who stop asking.
Come to the Sevenday Meeting. Auris temple, public hall. Candle-rise. Brother Edoran speaks.

If you carry knowledge of the original ritual - any piece, any fragment, any record of what Corven attempted and why - come to Brother Edoran directly. Do not wait. We cannot afford to wait. The city cannot afford to wait.

If you carry evidence that a second darkness is approaching, he will want to hear that more urgently still.
We are not afraid of hard truths. We have been sitting with the hardest one for fifty years. Come and sit with us. This is how the sun comes back.

Contact: Restorer Coordination Office, Highmark District. Ask for the Lantern-Saint.
On approach: Attend a Sevenday meeting. Introduce yourself. Ask questions before offering opinions - they have dealt with enough people who arrived with answers before they understood the problem. If you carry real evidence that the ritual can succeed, Brother Edoran will see you immediately. If you carry evidence that an enemy means to corrupt the Inversion rather than stop it, come at once. This is the news they have been dreading and waiting for in equal measure.
