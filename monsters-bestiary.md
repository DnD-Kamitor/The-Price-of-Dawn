## Faction 1: The Deluded Light Fanatics (The Reckoning)

> *"The sun does not rise because the Dawnborn hold its embers in their veins! Cut them open, and the sky will burn again!"*

The Reckoning is composed mostly of human citizens, city watch veterans, and desperate clergy who have tragically inverted the truth. They do not fight for malice or personal wealth—they genuinely believe they are martyrs saving Varenhold from freezing darkness. Led by the zealous Commander Harran Lecht, their discipline is ironclad, but their moral certainty can be shaken by pointing out civilian slaughter or showing them the true peaceful awakening of the sun.

::: {.callout-note}
### Battlefield Tactics & Moral Resolution
- **Shield Wall Formations:** Veterans and Militiamen advance in tight interlocking formations, granting each other +1 to +2 AC. Crossbow Marksmen provide suppressive cover fire to pin party spellcasters.
- **Lieutenant Senna Kard (The Conscience):** Senna can be persuaded to stand down with a successful DC 15 Persuasion check appealing to unnecessary slaughter. If she orders a ceasefire, her soldiers take the Dodge action.
- **Commander Harran Lecht (The Tragic Leader):** Cannot be persuaded with words alone. When reduced to 0 HP, witnessing the dawn circle awaken without human sacrifice shatters his delusion in tears.
:::

### Commander Harran Lecht
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

- **Armor Class:** 18 (scarred plate)
- **Hit Points:** 142 (15d8 + 75)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 18 (+4)
- **Saving Throws:** Str +9, Con +9, Wis +5, Cha +8
- **Skills:** Athletics +9, Intimidation +8, Perception +5
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, frightened
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** Common
- **Challenge:** 9 (5,000 XP)

**Traits:**
- **Desperate Martyrdom:** While below 70 HP (bloodied), Harran gains a +3 bonus to attack rolls, moves +10 ft faster, and deals an extra 9 (2d8) radiant damage on melee hits. He fights with terrifying righteous fury.
- **Iron Command Aura:** Allied soldiers within 30 ft add +1d6 to their attack rolls and saving throws, and cannot be frightened while Harran is conscious.
- **Legendary Fortitude (2/Day):** If Harran fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
- **The Unforgiving Choice:** Harran cannot be persuaded to abandon his attack while conscious; only reducing him to 0 HP or completing the stone circle before his eyes will shatter his delusion.

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Harran makes three attacks: two with his Sun-Forged Broadsword and one with his Shield Slam.
- **Sun-Forged Broadsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit (+12 if bloodied), reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing plus 4 (1d8) radiant damage (or 23 (4d8+5) when bloodied).
- **Solar Shield Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning. Target must succeed on a DC 17 Str save or be knocked prone and pushed 10 ft.

**Bonus Actions:**
- **Martyr's Rally (Recharge 5–6):** Harran roars: 'For the stolen sun!' Up to four allied soldiers within 30 ft gain 20 temporary hit points and can immediately use their reaction to make a melee weapon attack.

**Reactions:**
- **Unyielding Retaliation:** When hit by a melee attack, Harran adds +4 to his AC against that attack. If the attack still hits, he makes an immediate melee counterattack against the attacker.

**GM Tactics & Morale:**
> Charges directly for the center dais. Knocks party defenders prone and cleaves into backline casters to disrupt the dawn ritual. At bloodied (under 70 HP), he ignores opportunity attacks to reach the primer stone. When reduced to 0 HP, seeing the circle flare with pure sunlight shatters his fanatical delusion in tears.

**Bonus Actions:**
- **Commander's Re-alignment (Recharge 5–6):** Three allies within 30 ft disengage for free and move up to 20 ft.

**Reactions:**
- **Riposte & Disarm:** When a creature misses Senna with a melee attack, she makes a melee attack against that creature. On a hit, target drops its weapon (DC 15 Str save).

**GM Tactics & Morale:**
> Holds the secondary line 20 ft behind Harran, directing defensive maneuvers. If appealed to emotionally or shown civilian casualties, she can be persuaded (DC 16 Persuasion) to halt her soldiers.

## Faction 2: The Undead Faction (The Concordance — Ruled by Arch-Lich Vhol)

> *"In the stasis of shadow, there is no famine, no disease, no decay. The dawn is a fever of the flesh. We are the cure."*

The Concordance is an ancient necrotic deathless covenant ruled by **Arch-Lich Vhol**. They view the eternal twilight as their divine ascension and wage war using null-magic, Shadar-kai shadow-stalkers, and soul-draining inquisitors. Operating from the subterranean aqueducts, their purpose is to extinguish the sun forever by suppressing the ritual stone circles with obsidian null-plinthes.

::: {.callout-warning}
### Concordance Mechanics: Null Inversion & Lich Phylactery
- **Arch-Lich Vhol's Phylactery:** Hidden within the deep sub-aqueduct crypts behind three lead-lined ward seals. Vhol re-forms in 1d10 days unless his phylactery is crushed.
- **Null Inversion Aura:** The Null-Warden and Inversion Plinths project a 10-ft to 60-ft null-magic suppression zone requiring DC 15 Constitution saves to cast spells, dispelling light magic automatically at initiative count 20.
- **Pack Tactics & Paralysis:** The Returned ghasts swarm frontline combatants to inflict paralysis, enabling Unlit Operatives to shadow-step in for automatic critical sneak attacks.
:::

### Arch-Lich Vhol
*Medium undead, neutral evil*

- **Armor Class:** 18 (natural armor + unholy ward)
- **Hit Points:** 175 (22d8 + 76)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 22 (+6) | **WIS:** 18 (+4) | **CHA:** 18 (+4)
- **Saving Throws:** Con +10, Int +12, Wis +10, Cha +10
- **Skills:** Arcana +14, History +12, Insight +10, Perception +10
- **Damage Resistances:** Cold, lightning
- **Damage Immunities:** Necrotic, poison; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
- **Senses:** Truesight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Undercommon, Abyssal
- **Challenge:** 16 (15,000 XP)

**Traits:**
- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If Vhol fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
- **Rejuvenation (Phylactery):** If destroyed, gains a new body in 1d10 days unless his phylactery hidden in the aqueduct crypts is destroyed.
- **Turn Immunity:** Vhol is immune to effects that turn undead.
- **Inversion Nexus Aura:** Living creatures within 30 ft cannot regain HP. Any spell or radiant ability within 30 ft is corrupted: radiant damage becomes necrotic.

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Vhol uses Paralyzing Void-Touch and casts one cantrip or 1st-level spell.
- **Paralyzing Void-Touch:** Melee Spell Attack: +11 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 18 (4d6 + 4) cold plus 18 (4d6 + 4) necrotic. Target must succeed on DC 19 Con save or be paralyzed for 1 minute (save end of turn).
- **Null-Beam of the Void (Recharge 5–6):** 60-ft line, 10 ft wide. 55 (10d10) necrotic damage (DC 19 Dex half). Dispels all spell effects of 5th level or lower in the area.
- **Finger of Death (1/Day):** Target within 60 ft takes 61 (7d8 + 30) necrotic damage (DC 19 Con half). A humanoid killed rises immediately as a Concordance Ghast.
- **Spellcasting:** DC 19 spell save, +11 to hit. At-will: Toll the Dead (4d12), Shield (at will), Counterspell (3 slots, +6 check), Dispel Magic (3 slots), Blight (2 slots, 8d8 necrotic), Circle of Death (1 slot, 8d6 necrotic, 60-ft sphere).

**Bonus Actions:**
- **Void Step:** Teleports up to 30 ft to an unoccupied dark space.

**Reactions:**
- **Counterspell (3rd Slot, 3/Day):** Interrupts a spell of 3rd level or lower cast within 60 ft.

**GM Tactics & Morale:**
> Hovers 15 ft above the battlefield out of melee reach. Counters radiant spells immediately. Uses Null-Beam down the main line of PCs, then casts Circle of Death or Finger of Death on the primary healer/paladin. Uses Disrupt Life when surrounded.

## Faction 3: The Flowering (The Mutants)

> *"Why kneel to gods or dying stars when your flesh can grow wings, thorns, and hundred-year roots? Welcome the bloom."*

The Flowering is an illegal bio-magical flesh-shaping cult operating in the sunken, warm vent-works beneath the city. Led by **The Gardener**, they believe humanoid anatomy is a primitive limitation to be violently rewritten. They do not seek to destroy Varenhold, but to cultivate it. In combat, their primary objective is not mindless slaughter—they seek to capture the Dawnborn child Ixa, whom they revere as their 'first perfect bloom'.

::: {.callout-important}
### Flesh Horror Dynamics: Retrieval & "The Not Dead"
- **Target Lock on Ixa:** Grasping Tendrils and Abominations focus entirely on grappling and dragging Ixa toward sunken vent chutes (35 ft move speed). The party's victory condition is keeping Ixa within the defense perimeter.
- **The Not Dead Revelation:** Mutants reduced to 0 HP do not disintegrate; they collapse into twitching heaps. A DC 14 Medicine check reveals they are former Varenhold citizens painfully altered by mutagenic grafting, still conscious and aware inside.
:::
