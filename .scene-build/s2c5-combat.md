# S2C5 — Combat Structure: Harran's Arrival

<details>
<summary>⚔️ Combat</summary>

## Design Intent

This encounter is a clock problem, not an attrition problem. Players who treat it as a fight Harran and kill all eight Veterans will win the fight and may still lose the scene. The secondary objective — keeping Aldric at Stone 9 — is the actual measure of success. Make this clear by making Aldric's danger visible every round. Two Veterans are always working toward him. Always.

The encounter runs 3-5 combat rounds. Harran exits clean. This is not a boss fight. This is the first time players face the Reckoning directly, and the Reckoning always exits in good order.

---

## Initiative Setup

**Who rolls:** All player characters, Harran Lecht (Dex +2), Veterans as two groups: Drag Pair (roll once, Dex +1) and Combat Pair (roll once, Dex +1). Perimeter Pair does not act in combat unless charged.

**Surprise:** No surprise possible. Harran's column is visible for 30+ seconds before they reach the Ashring perimeter. Combat begins after failed negotiation or when Harran gives the order ("Take the stone.").

**Suggested initiative clusters:**

| Cluster | Creatures | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| High (18–20) | Fast PCs, possibly Harran | Harran is experienced and positioned deliberately |
| Mid (12–17) | Most PCs, Combat Veterans | Veterans roll once as a group |
| Low (8–11) | Drag Veterans, Harran if rolled low | Drag Pair moves toward Aldric; may be intercepted |

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM NOTE:** If the Drag Pair rolls high initiative, they reach Stone 9 before most players can react. This is intentional — it front-loads the tension and forces immediate positioning decisions. If the Drag Pair rolls low, players have a round to establish blocking positions. Both create different but valid encounters.
:::

## Round-by-Round Reckoning Tactics

### Before Round 1 — Harran's Opening Move

Harran does not attack immediately. He gives players exactly one exchange:

> "The Reckoning holds jurisdiction over the Ashring site under the Emergency Powers charter. Stand aside."

If players comply: they lose the scene. Aldric will be removed. Stone 9 dims.

If players resist or draw weapons: *"Take the stone."* — his order to the Drag Pair. Roll initiative.

---

### Round 1 — "Take the Stone"

**Drag Pair (V1, V2):** Move directly toward Stone 9. Their path runs approximately 40 ft. from the east approach to Stone 9. If no player is blocking them, they reach Aldric's position this round and attempt Drag as their bonus action immediately. If a player blocks their path, they engage that player (two shortsword attacks) before attempting Drag.

**Combat Pair (V3–V6):** Advance to engage the nearest player characters. Two players target the most forward PC. The other two hold position to cut off anyone trying to flank toward Stone 9.

**Harran:** Stays at range — approximately 30 ft. from the main player cluster. He does not engage in melee Round 1. He assesses. DC 14 Insight: *"He is watching which of you is most dangerous and which of you is most likely to reach Stone 9. He has already ranked you."*

**Aldric:** Braces at his stone. Uses his action to set himself defensively against Drag (his contested Athletics roll gains advantage this round — he knew it was coming). Says: *"Come on then."*

---

### Round 2 — Pressure

**Drag Pair:** If Aldric was not dragged Round 1 (players blocked or contested), both Veterans focus their full action on him — two shortsword attacks to soften him before the bonus action Drag attempt. Their priority is Drag, not damage, but they will deal damage to impair his ability to resist.

**Combat Pair:** Standard engagement. V3 and V4 focus on whichever player is most effectively protecting Aldric's approach lane. V5 and V6 continue general combat. If a player healer is visible and active, V5 redirects to disrupt healing.

**Harran:** If three or more players are engaged with Veterans, Harran closes to 15 ft. and makes his first Multiattack against the player closest to him. He targets last — he prefers to let Veterans soften targets. If Veterans are struggling (two down by end of Round 1), he closes sooner.

**Aldric:** Fights. Uses his action to attack the Drag Pair Veteran nearest him (shortsword +4, 1d6+2). His bonus action is used to brace against Drag if he senses it coming.

---

### Round 3 — The Tipping Point

This is when the encounter reveals which direction it's going.

**If Aldric is still on his stone:** Harran has not achieved his objective. The Drag Pair is under pressure. He uses his Inspiring Shout this round if three Veterans have been dropped — the remaining allies regain 10 HP and push. If not three down yet, he continues standard engagement.

**If Aldric was pulled off his stone:** Stone 9 dims. The Drag Pair holds position near Stone 9 to prevent Aldric from returning. Harran's energy level shifts slightly — he hasn't won but he's moved toward his objective. Combat continues.

**Inspiring Shout trigger (Recharge 5-6, but use it Round 3 if triggered):** When three Veterans are down, Harran calls out sharply — not a rallying shout, a field command, the specific short phrase that veterans of the Reckoning's training know means *"regroup and hold."* Read aloud:

> *"Hold your line."*

*No drama. No passion. A flat command from a man who expects it to work because it always has. The three Veterans still standing pause fractionally — you see it in their shoulders — and then re-engage harder than they were before.*

The surviving Veterans regain 10 HP each. If only one or two survive to this point, the math shifts significantly.

---

### Round 4-5 — Exit Sequence

**Harran's exit condition triggers when:**
- He reaches 32 HP or fewer (half HP), OR
- Three sides of his current position are occupied by hostile creatures

**Tactical Retreat sequence:**
1. Harran takes a step back — not a panicked retreat, a measured step. One foot, weight shifted.
2. He looks at the player who dealt the most recent damage to him. Directly. For one second.
3. He says the exit line (see below). Not loudly. At normal speaking volume.
4. Tactical Retreat fires: Harran and any conscious Veterans within 10 ft. Disengage and move 30 ft. without opportunity attacks.
5. The Perimeter Pair at the east entrance falls back with them.
6. They leave in formation. Not running.

**Exit line (mandatory — do not let Harran die here):**
> "When the city collapses in six months, tell me which principle saved it."

*Then he walks away.*

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM NOTE — THE EXIT IS NOT A DEFEAT:** Harran does not lose this encounter the way a typical enemy loses a fight. His exit is tactical. He has accomplished partial information (he now knows players will defend the Ashring actively, which changes his planning) and partial pressure (if Aldric was pulled, even briefly, that matters). He will be back, differently. The exit line is not sour grapes — it is a genuine question he will still be asking in Session 5. Do not editorialize after he says it. Let it sit.
:::

## Secondary Objective Tracker

Track the state of Aldric's position each round.

| Round | Drag Pair Status | Aldric's Status | Stone 9 Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Moving toward Stone 9 | At stone, braced | Lit (normal) |
| Round 1 | At stone or blocked | Contested or free | Lit |
| Round 2 | Engaged with Aldric | Contested or pulled | Lit / Dimming |
| Round 3 | Pressing or repulsed | On stone / Off stone | Lit / Dim |
| Round 4+ | Per above | Per above | Per above |

**Aldric pulled off stone:** Stone 9 dims immediately. If Aldric returns to the stone within the same combat (players get him back), he can re-attune — requires his full action to place his hand on the stone, plus one round of contact. Stone re-lights at end of that round. He is rattled.

**Aldric off stone at combat end:** Stone 9 remains dim. Players can encourage Aldric to return, but he needs a moment — he was physically removed from the thing he has been standing at for months. He will return if players are with him. He will not return if he is alone.

## Harran's Positioning — Round by Round

| Round | Harran's Position | Range from Players |
|---|---|---|
| Before combat | East approach, center | 50 ft. |
| Round 1 | Advancing to 30 ft. | 30 ft. — assessing |
| Round 2 | Closing to 15 ft. | 15 ft. — Multiattack |
| Round 3 | Engaged or near-engaged | Melee or 5-10 ft. |
| Round 4/Exit | Tactical Retreat begins | Moving to 30+ ft., then away |

Harran targets the player between him and Stone 9 first — not because they're the biggest threat, but because they're in the way of the lane he's managing. Second priority: whichever player is most effectively coordinating the Aldric defense.

---

## What Aldric Does in Combat

Aldric fights. He is a retired guardsman with thirty-one years of service and chronic knee problems. He is not a fragile old man. He is a person with bad knees who has made a decision.

- **Priority 1:** Stay on the stone. He will use his action to brace/resist Drag before attacking.
- **Priority 2:** Attack the Veteran trying to Drag him. Two attacks if able (+4, 1d6+2 each).
- **Priority 3:** Call out to players when Veterans get around them toward his stone: *"Two coming west — I see them."*

**If a player joins him at Stone 9 to help defend it:** He gives that player a look — not gratitude, not surprise, just acknowledgment — and then focuses on the fight. He has been waiting for this.

**If he gets pulled off the stone:** He says *"I know what happens if I leave it"* as he goes — as he's being physically moved — not to Harran, to himself. He will fight to return immediately. His next action, every action, is moving back to Stone 9.

## Complications Table (d6)

Roll once at the end of Round 2 if the scene needs texture:

| d6 | Complication |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Stone 7 resonates:** One of the unoccupied Primer Stones flares bright amber — a response to the Drag attempt, not to any player action. Harran notices. His eyes go to it. DC 12 Insight: he's recalculating something. He doesn't know the stones respond to Drag proximity. Now he does. |
| 2 | **Aldric's knee:** He goes down on one leg mid-round — chronic pain, bad timing. He doesn't fall, but he's half-kneeling when the Drag Pair reaches him. His contested Athletics this round has disadvantage. He says nothing about it. |
| 3 | **Harran's documentation:** A folded charter paper falls from Harran's coat pocket when he takes a hit. It lands on the flagstones 5 ft. away. He doesn't go for it. He can get it after. Players can intercept it as an object interaction (free). Contents: the Emergency Powers charter with Harran's jurisdiction clauses annotated in his own hand. Reveals his actual legal standing (and its limits). |
| 4 | **The perimeter pair advances:** V7 and V8, seeing the combat going longer than expected, begin advancing from the east road entrance. They reach the perimeter of the Ashring in 2 rounds. Players now have a flanking threat to manage. |
| 5 | **A torch goes out:** One of the amber-infused torches at the circle's edge gutters and dies — the stone nearest it dims slightly in response. Not Stone 9, but a reminder that the Ashring is sensitive to disruption. Combat in the dark at the stone edges creates difficult terrain (that 10 ft. zone goes to dim light). |
| 6 | **Aldric speaks to Harran:** *"I served under you in the East Gate riots, Commander. I know you don't need to do this this way."* Harran turns to look at him for one second. His hand goes to his sword hilt — not to draw, just contact. Then: *"That was eleven years ago, Stone."* He doesn't stop. But the hesitation was real. |
