## Social Mechanics

---

### Are There Rolls?

This scene is not gated by dice. Theron has decided to tell the truth before the players arrive in this room. No Persuasion check compels the confession. No Intimidation unlocks information that kindness doesn't. He is past the point where social mechanics operate on him in the usual direction.

The rolls that exist here are **Insight** checks — not to extract information but to understand what is underneath what Theron is saying.

---

### Insight: What Theron Reveals at Each DC

All information below is available to a character who rolls Insight after Theron has spoken the relevant content. These are not gating rolls — a perceptive player who asks good questions gets the same result without rolling.

| DC | What a player notices |
|---|---|
| **10** | Theron is not performing composure. The control he showed in Scenes 2 and 3 is genuinely gone. This is not a strategic choice — he has run out of the energy performance requires. |
| **13** | The word EXTINGUISHED costs him something to say. Not because he has not thought it — he has thought it for eleven years — but because saying it out loud in this room, in front of people who now know what it means, is a different act from thinking it. He does not show this on his face. It is in the pause before and the quiet after. |
| **15** | Theron is watching how the players receive the information. He is specifically watching for whether they name the Dawnborn as people rather than as a mechanism. Their first response to the word EXTINGUISHED tells him something he has been waiting to learn for eleven years: what kind of people found this. |
| **17** | His claim that he was "protecting people" is partly true and partly not. The part that is not true: he was protecting himself from being the person who said the word *extinguished* to another human being. He knows this. If pressed, he will admit it — but he will not volunteer it. He is tired, not dishonest. |
| **20** | The note-burning (his destroyed notes about the Notation Key) was not security practice. It was an attempt to remove his own ability to decode the rest of the text. It failed. He remembers every page. The act of destroying them made them permanent. He has lived with the irony of this. |

### If Players Try to Charm or Pressure Theron

There is nothing to extract here that he is not already giving. If a player attempts Persuasion or Intimidation:

**Persuasion:** Theron is not resistant. But flattery or social pressure does not accelerate what he is doing — he is moving at the pace of honesty, which has its own speed. He does not respond to charm. He responds to direct questions.

**Intimidation:** He looks at them. His expression does not change. He says: *"I have been frightening myself with this information for eleven years. You will need to do something I haven't managed."* It is not defiance — it is a simple statement of fact about the relative scale of threats.

### Isolde Maren — Information Packet

If players ask about Isolde or seek her location, Theron provides the following. No roll required.

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Isolde Maren |
| **Occupation** | Independent ritual researcher; formerly held a junior research appointment at the Amber Spire (revoked by Keseph Vyne, "institutional restructuring") |
| **What she's researching** | Transfer method: moving anchor energy from living vessels (the Dawnborn) into constructed vessels (objects — specifically, polished amber with specific alchemical preparation). If successful, the ritual could complete without the Dawnborn releasing their energy into open air. The vessels absorb it. |
| **Current status** | Working from personal notes in a rented room. No institutional support. No lab equipment beyond what she carried out in a case when her space was reassigned. |
| **Location** | Weavers' Terrace, Dusk Parishes, Ashring district. Third floor. The room with the east-facing window — she works by natural light when there is any. |
| **Theron's history with her** | He gave her unrestricted access to the lower Archive stacks three years ago, bypassing the standard institutional review process. Keseph Vyne discovered this and revoked the access within six weeks. Isolde lost access to two primary sources she had not yet transcribed. Theron arranged for a transcriptionist to copy three additional pages before the revocation was enforced. He sent these without identifying himself. He does not know if she received them. |
| **Current viability** | Theron believes her method is theoretically sound. He does not know if it works in practice. He has not had access to her current notes. The question is whether she has enough remaining source material to complete the proof. |
| **What players should know before meeting her** | Keseph Vyne knows who she is and has been watching her work. She has not been harmed — harming her would draw attention — but her access has been systematically cut off. She is aware she is being managed. She is also not stopped. |

### Fragment 2 of the Notation Key

Also present in the box. Players may have noticed it or may ask about it now.

**What it is:** A partial cipher reference for Corven's private notation system. Combined with Fragment 1 (from Session 1, Shelf 4-17-3), players can decode approximately 60% of Corven's private journal entries. Full decoding requires Fragment 3, which is in Session 3.

**Theron on it:** If asked, he says:

> "That is the second piece of the Key. You'll need the third. I don't know where it is — Corven hid it himself. I have theories."

He does have theories. He will share them if pressed. One is accurate: he believes it is in the ritual site at the Ashring.

**What combined Fragment 1+2 unlocks:** The consent passages in Corven's journal. The entry from the night before the ritual. A partial record of which ten children were chosen and the word Corven used for what they carried: *vessels of unwilling dawn.* He meant they did not choose this. He regretted that. The journal says so, in partially decodable notation.
