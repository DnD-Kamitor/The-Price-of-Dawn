## Edoran Relationship States (Three Variants)

---

### Variant A — Full Engagement

**Conditions:** Players approached, stayed, and someone asked the daughter question — or otherwise found the grief beneath the theology through genuine engagement rather than investigation technique.

**State:** Edoran knows who these players are. Not as investigators from the Chancellor's office — as people. He registered something specific about each of them in this conversation, the way a serious person registers the specific qualities of someone who surprised them.

**In Sessions 3-5:** He will speak to them directly when it matters. He will not wait for them to come to him — if something happens that the players need to know about before they stumble into it, he will find a way to reach them. This will happen exactly once, in Session 4, when he intervenes in a situation they cannot fully understand yet. He will not explain the intervention. They will understand it later.

**The specific thing he gives only to Variant A parties:** In Session 4, or whenever players learn about the Inversion pathway, he will tell them — if they come to him — why he has been sitting on Fragment 2 for twelve years. The reason is not strategic. The reason is: *"I found it two years after Mira died. If it is true — if all ten genuinely consent — then there was never a reason for any of it. The grey sickness, the waiting, the fifty years of darkness. There was a door and nobody found it. I have been sitting with whether the door is real for twelve years. I don't know what to do with it yet."* This is not something he tells anyone who has not found the crack.

**In the closing ritual (Session 5):** His Restorer followers are present. They do not interfere. He has instructed them that genuine consent is the requirement, not facilitated completion.

---

### Variant B — Adequate

**Conditions:** Players approached and talked. They did not find the crack, did not ask anything personal, but they engaged with the argument and heard the living conduit beat and the consent argument.

**State:** Edoran knows who they are. He is watching. He does not feel known in return, which matters less to him than to most people — he is used to having conversations where the substance was exchanged and the people remained opaque — but it registers.

**In Sessions 3-5:** He watches. He does not reach out. If players come to him with a specific question, he answers it fully and with the same precision he used tonight. He does not volunteer more than asked. If players are in a difficult situation that he could resolve by intervening, he waits to see if they can resolve it themselves. He may intervene once in Session 4 — not on their behalf specifically, but in a way that benefits them, for reasons connected to his own purposes.

**In the closing ritual (Session 5):** His Restorer followers attend. He does not interfere. He is watching to see how the consent question resolves.

---

### Variant C — Avoided or Fought

**Conditions:** Players bypassed Edoran using the western passage, ignored him entirely, or engaged in combat.

**State:** He notes what kind of people these are. Not with contempt — he has been doing this for twenty years and he knows what investigators look like when they are not yet ready for a conversation. He is patient about readiness. He can wait.

**In Sessions 2-3:** The Restorers become more visible. The zealots from S1C1 — the ones who were explicitly stood down tonight — reappear in Session 2, not as an intercept but as a visible presence. Players will know they are being watched. Edoran's theology hardens in how it is communicated: the movement becomes less patient, less careful about the consent-versus-coercion line, because Edoran himself is less sure these players understand the distinction or will act on it.

**In Sessions 4-5:** He is still there. He is still right about several things. The conversation he did not have with players in S1C5 remains available — but it costs something to have now, because he has made other decisions in the interim based on the assumption that these players were not going to be part of a thoughtful resolution. One of those decisions may have downstream costs.

## Session 2 Hooks (Planted in This Scene)

**Hook 1: The Archive Has More**

Edoran said it directly. He does not say what. Players who go back to the Archive in Session 2 are not chasing a vague instruction — they are pursuing a specific gap in their information that a well-sourced antagonist told them exists. Fragment 2 of the Notation Key is the answer. Finding it changes the picture entirely.

**Hook 2: "Tonight"**

The exit line: *"The Restorers will not trouble you again tonight."* The word "tonight" is a promise with a temporal limit. Players who noted the word know the Restorers will be present again. Players who didn't note it will register it when the Restorers reappear in Session 2. Either way: the thread was laid here.

**Hook 3: The Grief Beneath the Theology**

Players who found the crack — or who registered at DC 13 Insight that something personal built this — have a question about Edoran that is not answered tonight. Who was she? What did her death cost, specifically? The mortality records for fourteen years ago are public archive. The gap between that death and the founding of the Restorers is two years. The thread leads to a human being, not a movement leader, and understanding the human being changes how everything Edoran says in Sessions 3-4 lands.
