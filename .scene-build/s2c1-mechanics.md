## Partial Save Outcomes

### 1 Bundle Saved

The players preserved something, but the loss is real. The missing bundles create a specific gap in Scene 3.

- If Bundle A is lost: WREN's colophon is gone. The third witness to Corven's notation cannot be confirmed through documents alone. Players must find another path to WREN — via Theron's verbal testimony or through the slips.
- If Bundle B is lost: Keseph Vyne's name does not surface in this scene. The bureaucratic thread is severed. Players may still find it later, but the Scene 2 conversation with Theron will not have documentary confirmation to anchor it.
- If Bundle C is lost: Two colophons (CORVEN and SALI) are gone. The resonance box puzzle in Scene 3 has one missing voice sequence. The box can still be activated but requires a different approach (see `session2.md`).

### 2 Bundles Saved

Standard outcome. Players have enough to continue. Theron emerges at normal pace. One gap exists (per whichever bundle was lost) but is workable.

### All 3 Bundles Saved

Bonus outcome. Theron responds to competence — he emerges from the alcove faster (immediately, rather than 1d4 minutes) and is slightly more composed. He has watched people act to preserve what matters. That shifts something in him.

Additionally: players find a loose folio that had slipped from Bundle B during the chaos — a handwritten note in the margins of Keseph's correspondence: *"If the players of the Archive reach the witnesses before we do, advise secondary contact."* The word "players" is bureaucratic jargon for outside investigators. The note confirms someone was tracking their progress before this scene.
