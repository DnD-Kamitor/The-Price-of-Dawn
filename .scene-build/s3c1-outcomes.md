# S3C1 — Lowmark Alley: Scene Results & Forward Hooks

<details><summary>✅ Outcome 1: De-escalated — Senna Heard Something True</summary>

**What happened:** Players engaged Senna directly. They reached her crack — named Nia and Brek, or acknowledged that the ritual might not work even if the Dawnborn die, or both. She said: *"If they die for this, I need to know they chose it."* She stepped back. The crowd parted.

**Immediate consequences:**
- Players reach Isolde's door without friction
- Isolde sees the exchange from the window; opens the door herself
- Her first line: *"I saw what you did. With the woman outside. Come in."*
- No penalty to Transfer Apparatus checks in S3C3

**Senna — between sessions:**
She goes home. She makes sure Nia ate. She thinks about what was said to her. The specific thing: that someone might have been working on a way to save them, all of them, and the Dawnhalls knew about it and suppressed it. She doesn't have language for that yet. She will by Session 5.

**Session 5 — Ashring Plaza:**
Senna arrives with forty people. They stand at the plaza edge. They are witnesses. Not protesters — witnesses. Her presence tells the crowd that what's happening matters, that it's real, that someone who has been paying attention since before the players arrived has decided to see it through. When pressure builds in S3C5 (and it will), Senna's crowd keeps the plaza from tipping. If players recognize her and speak to her: *"I brought people to see. Whatever happens here — someone should see it."*

She does not say she trusts them. She says she is there to see. That's enough.

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM:** The difference between forty witnesses and a mob can determine whether S3C5's climax is survivable. Senna's presence in the de-escalated outcome is real mechanical support — not combat, not skills, but crowd dynamics. Her people listen to her. If she holds, the plaza holds.
:::

</details>

<details><summary>🔀 Outcome 2: Flanked — Players Found the Side Lane</summary>

**What happened:** Players spotted the side lane north of the crowd. They bypassed Senna and the crowd entirely, reached the building's north side, accessed Isolde's stairwell without confrontation.

**Immediate consequences:**
- Players reach Isolde's door without engaging Senna
- Isolde does not see them coming (they approached from north, not through the crowd)
- She opens the door at their knock; she is cautious
- No Transfer Apparatus penalty

**Senna — between scenes:**
The crowd disperses on its own within an hour. Nothing happened. Nobody came down. Nobody acknowledged them. Senna picks up her placard and carries it home. She doesn't know who the players are. She didn't see them.

**Session 5 — Ashring Plaza:**
Senna appears as an unknown variable. She has her usual core group — fifteen, twenty people. She doesn't know the players. If players approach her, she responds to their approach on its own terms — she has no prior opinion of them. She can still be the stabilizing force in S3C5, but it requires players to build that connection in the moment rather than having it already established.

The stakes of the connection-building are higher in Session 5 with less time. The scene can still work. It just requires more from the players.

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM:** The flanked outcome is not a failure — it's a choice to avoid the problem rather than solve it. That choice has a cost measured in Session 5 preparation time. Some tables prefer the clean approach. Let them. The campaign tracks it.
:::

</details>

<details><summary>💥 Outcome 3: Forced Through — Combat Path Taken</summary>

**What happened:** Players pushed through the crowd. The phase checks went badly, or players chose not to attempt them. The crowd scattered. Senna backed to the far edge, watching. The window went dark. Players reached the door.

**Immediate consequences:**
- Isolde's window was dark when players reached the door
- Isolde is shaken when players arrive upstairs
- Her first line: *"I heard the noise. I thought it was the Reckoning."*
- +1 difficulty to first Transfer Apparatus check in S3C3 (Isolde's hands are not steady)

**Senna — between scenes:**
She stays until the alley is clear. She is the last to leave. She picks up her placard — it fell during the crowd's scatter — and carries it away. She does not speak.

**Session 5 — Ashring Plaza:**
Senna is there. Not with forty people — maybe six or eight. She is watching. She has not organized a witness crowd because she doesn't know if these players are worth witnessing. She wants to find out.

If players in S3C5 demonstrate that they are actually trying to give the Dawnborn a choice — that the consent question is real, that they mean it — Senna says, once: *"So you learned something."*

She doesn't say it gratefully. She says it like a verdict. Like she has been holding the question open since Lowmark Alley and she has just received an answer.

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM:** The forced-through outcome has the highest narrative weight in Session 5. Senna showing up with six people instead of forty is a visual statement — players can count. If they forced through Lowmark Alley and made no effort to address it between sessions, they will see the difference. If they find Senna before S3C5 and do the work they skipped, the crowd can grow — but it will take player action.
:::

</details>

<details><summary>🔮 In All Outcomes: Davan Crel's Report</summary>

Regardless of what players do or don't do about Davan Crel, his report has been sent. The raid in Session 3, Scene 5 happens.

**If players ignored him:** They will not understand the raid as anything other than reactive until they reconstruct the timeline. In S3C5 or S4, they can figure out that the Amber Compact was watching from before Session 3 began.

**If players noticed him but left him:** They know someone was watching. They may have increased operational security or warned Isolde. The raid still comes, but players are less surprised.

**If players confronted him:** He lied. He left. The raid still comes. Players have more reason to suspect the Amber Compact's active involvement.

**If players detained or incapacitated him:** The report was already sent. Someone else confirms the players' arrival. Davan's absence is itself a data point. Vyne knows players are actively interfering now.

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM:** Make sure S3C5's raid is not presented as a surprise that "came from nowhere." Players who paid attention have threads. Players who didn't have the experience of learning why the threads mattered. Both are valid scene designs.
:::

</details>

<details><summary>🔮 Session 3 Forward Hooks from This Scene</summary>

**Hook 1 — Davan Crel as Amber Compact informant:**
In S3C5, when Harran Lecht's soldiers arrive, players who noticed Davan will have a framework. The Amber Compact has been ahead of them since the raid on Isolde's address. The players are not the only people who have been doing research. This recontextualizes everything from Session 2 onward — their investigation has been observed the whole time.

**Hook 2 — Senna's daughters' names:**
Nia and Brek. Nine and six. Early grey sickness symptoms. These names will echo in Sessions 4-5 when Lira Anwick's daughter enters the story. Two mothers. Two daughters. Both affected by the same disease, on opposite sides of the consent question. Lira refuses to die because of her daughter; Senna is here because of her daughters. Players who remember Nia and Brek will hear the structural parallel when Lira's daughter's name appears.

**Hook 3 — "Did they choose?" as the campaign question:**
Senna asked it in Lowmark Alley before players had the language for it. *"If they die for this, I need to know they chose it."* This is the consent-as-requirement position. It is Edoran's position, articulated from grief instead of theology. It is what the Inversion possibility turns on. Players who heard Senna say it will recognize, in Session 4, that they've been building toward answering her question all along.
