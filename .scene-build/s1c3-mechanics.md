## 1. Scene Context

**Location:** Restricted Stacks, 4th floor, Stack 4-17. Long narrow room (80×30 ft), shelves floor-to-ceiling, sourceless amber light, ribbed skylight showing Varenhold's permanent dusk stars overhead, brass constellation plaques on each shelf section, central column with 3-ring star-wheel mechanism.

**Party state:** Gestalt Level 3. Two class progressions, one action economy. ~35 HP, AC 15–18. Passive Perception typically 13–15.

**Key NPC present:** Theron Waide — Master Archivist. Has known the star-wheel's answer for eleven years, has never opened shelf 4-17-3. Carries guilt, relief, and shame in simultaneous suspension. Will watch the mechanism. Will not touch it.

**Corven:** Archmagister, died 50 years ago. Present only through objects: the mechanism, the star chart, the handwriting on shelf labels, the notation key fragment. His personality is legible from his design choices; his voice is in his written glosses.

**Scope of this tab:** All checks NOT covered in s1c3-combat.md (which handles star-wheel puzzle mechanics, ring configuration, and the failure rotation sequence). This tab covers passive knowledge on entering, Theron's body language, the contents of shelf 4-17-3, the notation key fragment, the public notes document, the cedar smell, the skylight, the star chart as character study, and the footstep sequence.

## 4. The Public Notes Document — What Players Can Extract

**Physical state:** A document tube from shelf 4-17-3, sealed with wax. Label reads: *Public Summary — Ritual Architecture and Sympathetic Mechanism — For Archive Record.* Players must open the tube and unroll the document.

**What is present:**

The document is approximately six pages of dense technical description covering the ritual's architecture:
- The general model of a distributed sympathetic anchor system across a defined geographic area
- The concept of a living conduit mechanism as the ritual's central operational component
- The ritual's intended long-term stability properties (described as self-sustaining once established)
- A brief reference to the consent requirement as a structural specification: *"the willing participation of each component element is not a procedural safeguard but a functional requirement of the sympathetic bond"*

**What it implies (if players make appropriate checks — see DC table above):**

- The ritual has multiple anchor points, not one
- The anchor points are sympathetic, not physical — they must be maintained through resonance, not construction
- The word "living" in "living conduit mechanism" is doing specific technical work
- The consent requirement was structural, not moral — the ritual fails without it
- The document was written for a technically sophisticated audience and does not define its terms

**What it withholds (until Session 2 and beyond):**

The document does not name or describe:
- What or who the living conduits are
- How many anchors exist and where they are placed
- What the ongoing cost to the anchor components is
- What happens if an anchor is removed
- Whether the living conduit mechanism is still operational
- What "distributed" means in terms of specific geographic placement

**The phrase to flag, not explain:** "Living conduit mechanism." When players encounter this phrase, do not gloss it. Let them carry the word "living" forward. If asked directly, Theron says: *"Corven used 'living' in a specific technical sense. I believe it is defined in the later fragments."* He is telling the truth. He is also relieved not to say more.
