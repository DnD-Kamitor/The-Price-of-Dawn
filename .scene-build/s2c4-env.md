## Theron's Study — Scene 4 State

The room has not changed. The change is in what is now on the desk.

The resonance box is open. It sits exactly where it was when the lid came up — Theron has not moved it, has not touched it since. The lid rests back at an angle, the internal silk lining visible, the faint scorch marks from the broken seal (if forced) or the clean joint of the mechanism (if opened correctly) catching the lamplight. The box is not the focus of the room anymore. It has done what it was built to do.

Corven's pages are in the players' hands, or on the desk in front of them. They are not ancient in the way of crumbling vellum — Theron has kept them well. They are simply old, and dense, and the ink in the third page still carries weight even now: *"The completion requires their release."*

Fragment 2 of the Notation Key is also present. It sits separately from the main pages. Players may have noticed it or may not have yet. Theron has not drawn attention to it. He will if asked.

## The Transition Beat

Scene 3 ended with a puzzle completed and a box opened. Scene 4 does not begin with energy. It begins with the space after energy.

The players have just done the thing. They've spoken the names, pressed the indentations, watched the lid come free. They have held the pages. They have read the passage. The room did not catch fire. No alarm sounded. The terrible thing is simply present, in ordinary ink, in a man's handwriting, and it says what it says.

Theron is standing where he was when the lid opened. Not at his desk. Not in his chair. He is standing slightly apart from both, near the gap in the shelves where the box used to live. He has not sat down. He has not moved toward the players. He is simply standing, facing the room, waiting for one of them to look up from the pages.

When a player looks up: that is the opening beat. Theron holds their eyes for a moment. Then he says:

> "I need to tell you something about how long I have known."

## Five Senses

**Sight:** The box open on the desk, lid tilted back. Corven's pages — three of them, written in a precise, slightly cramped hand. Fragment 2 of the Notation Key, set to one side, smaller, a single page folded twice. Theron standing near the shelf gap, still in his work coat. The lamp has not moved. The room is slightly less warm than it was when the box was sealed — whatever the mechanism held has dispersed.

**Sound:** The Archive is empty below them. The soldiers are gone. The study carries the ambient sound of old wood: faint settling, the creak of the shelf behind Theron when he shifts his weight. At some point in this scene Theron sits — in the plain chair across from the desk, not behind it. The sound of the chair when he sits is too loud in the silence. Neither he nor the players acknowledge it.

**Smell:** Old wood. Old paper. The same smell as Scene 3, but the faint warmth that came from the box's sealed mechanism has faded. If the box was forced open, there is also a thin ghost of scorch from the broken seal — not smoke exactly, more the smell of heated metal after it cools. The room smells like what has just ended.

**Touch:** The pages in the players' hands are cool. Not cold — room temperature, the ordinary coolness of something that has been sealed away from warmth for eleven years. The paper is slightly heavier than standard Archive document stock. Corven used good materials. Players who are holding the pages during Theron's confession are holding the physical object that contains the sentence.

**Taste:** This is the sense to redirect toward the players. Ask them, directly or by implication, what their character is feeling. The sense you invite them into rather than describe.

## What Players Are Holding

The key page is page three of Corven's original redirection notes. Players have already read it to get here. The operative sentence:

> *"The anchors are not lost. The light did not die. It redistributed into ten living vessels born at the moment of inversion. The ritual is incomplete, not failed. The completion requires their release."*

The word *release* is doing work that has not yet been named. Theron is about to name it.
