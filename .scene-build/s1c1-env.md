## 1. Tactical Map Description

### The Lowmark Junction — ~50×50 ft

A cramped four-way street junction in the lower residential Lowmark, roughly 50 ft across where two cobblestone lanes intersect. The streets are older than the rest of the city and show it: worn stone, iron lantern posts bolted into the corners of buildings, second-story washing lines overhead. The permanent amber twilight is thickest here — lamplight pools low on the cobbles and does not reach the upper storeys.

---

### Key Features

| Feature | Location | Tactical Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Food Registry Queue** | South to east wall, ~20 ft long | Difficult terrain; 10–15 bundled civilians standing close. Provides half cover from the waist up while inside it. Moving through costs 2 ft per 1 ft. Players entering from the south pass along its western edge. |
| **Distribution Cart** | Center-south, flanking the queue | A heavy four-wheeled cart loaded with cloth-wrapped food parcels. Blocking terrain — cannot be moved in combat. Large enough to crouch behind for three-quarters cover. Sits on the lane centerline. |
| **Market Crates** | East side, against the building wall | Stacked 3–4 high, can be upended as a bonus action (Str DC 10) to create a 5 ft wide low barricade (half cover) or scattered difficult terrain in a 10 ft square. **The sealed note lands here** if the zealot is hit before round 2 ends. |
| **Lantern Posts** | NW corner, NE corner, SE corner | Iron posts 6 ft tall with oil-burning lanterns. A lantern can be knocked from its bracket (DC 12 Str or a successful attack on the post, AC 10, 1 HP) — it falls and shatters, creating a 5-ft fire hazard (1d4 fire damage per turn to anyone entering, lasts 3 rounds). **The NE post is the note-burn point**: if a zealot reaches it, they can attempt to touch the sealed note to the flame as a bonus action from round 2 onward. |
| **N Alley Mouth** | North-center | 10 ft wide. Exit route for zealots if the fight collapses. No civilians present. Connects to the Spire Quarter road 200 ft north. |
| **E Alley Mouth** | East-center | 10 ft wide. Edoran is 30 ft into this alley, motionless in shadow. The alley curves left at 40 ft, becoming invisible from the junction. |
| **S Lane** | South edge (players enter here) | The lane the players arrive from; the food queue begins approximately 5 ft inside the junction on the eastern side of this approach. |
| **W Alley Mouth** | West-center | 10 ft wide. One zealot covers this exit. Connects to a residential lane; no tactical significance beyond blocking retreat. |

**Zealot placement summary:**
- Z1 (South): Near the tail of the food queue, ~5 ft into the junction from the south edge; cuts off easy retreat
- Z2 (West alley mouth): Blocks the western exit; 15 ft from center
- Z3 (North, flanking Enforcer): ~20 ft from center, 5 ft east of the north lane
- Z4 (East-north, near crates): ~15 ft from center, positioned between the crates and the NE lantern post — **this is the note-carrier**
- Enforcer: Center-north, 20 ft from the distribution cart, directly on the route to the Archive; facing south toward the players

**Edoran:** 30 ft east into the east alley. Standing completely still in shadow. Not visible on a casual glance. DC 15 Perception (or auto at range 30 ft with darkvision past the alley mouth) to notice a figure that is not moving like anyone else in the crowd.

## 2. Three Read-Aloud Variants

### A — Cautious (Party scanning, moving carefully)

> The junction is busier than it should be at this hour. The food queue stretches from the distribution cart all the way to the east wall — fifteen people, maybe more, heads down, breath fogging in the amber half-light. You're tracking the lanterns, the exits, the shadows, which is why you see it first: four figures who are not in the queue, not moving toward it, not doing anything a person in the Lowmark has any reason to do. They are waiting. They are waiting specifically for you.

---

### B — Rushing (Party moving fast, focused on the Archive)

> The quickest route through the junction cuts past the food queue — you can see the distribution cart, the crates, the north lane that leads straight up toward the Archive. You're three steps into the intersection when you hear it: boots on cobblestone, fast and deliberate, from three directions at once. The crowd in the queue goes still. Someone grabs a child by the arm. A figure in the center of the north lane spreads his hands slowly, palms out, blocking the road. He is not asking.

---

### C — Split Party (Party spread across the junction)

> The junction opens in front of you — some of you near the distribution cart, some cutting wide around the queue's end, someone already past the crates on the eastern side. Which is why you all notice it at slightly different moments. The stillness at the west alley mouth. The man near the lantern post who is not looking at the queue. The figure in the center of the road north who has been there since before you arrived. The gap closes differently for each of you. There is nowhere convenient to regroup.

## 3. Five Senses — Expanded

### Before the Ambush: The Mundane Scene

**Sight**
- Amber lanternlight lying flat on wet cobblestones, stretching long shadows from the cart wheels and the queue's shuffling feet
- Bundled figures in the food line — coats, scarves, the particular hunched patience of people who have been waiting in cold air for a long time
- The NE lantern post flame moving slightly in a draft from the east alley, catching the metallic clasp of a belt pouch on a figure not standing in the queue

**Sound**
- Low murmur from the line — not conversation exactly, more the sound of people keeping quiet together
- A vendor's voice calling quantities: *"One allocation per name — show your registry mark at the cart"*
- Cobblestone echo from the north lane carrying traffic noise from the wider city somewhere beyond

**Smell**
- Lamp oil, thick and slightly sweet — the particular smell of amber lantern fuel that is everywhere in the Lowmark
- Old bread and something that might be preserved root vegetables from the distribution cart
- River cold from the lower streets, carrying the iron smell that clings to the Lowmark in all seasons

**Touch**
- Cold air on exposed skin — the permanent damp of a city at artificial twilight, never fully warm
- Cobblestones underfoot, worn smooth in the center, rough at the edges where cart wheels don't reach
- The slight press of passing foot traffic from the queue on the players' south side as they enter

**Taste**
- Dust and cold air, the particular mineral dryness that fills every sealed building in Varenhold — the taste of a city that has been indoors for fifty years, even when you're outside

---

### The Moment It Starts: The Threat Shift

**Sight**
- The queue goes still — not all at once, but like a wave passing through the line, people stopping mid-shuffle — because the people not in the queue are moving
- The figure at the north lane spreads his hands: no weapon visible, which is worse than a weapon
- A glint at the NE lantern post as the note-carrier's hand drops to his belt pouch

**Sound**
- The vendor voice cuts off mid-call
- Boot leather on cobblestone — fast and deliberate — from west, south, and north simultaneously; the sound of people who know where they're going
- A child in the queue says something; the parent pulls them close and says nothing

**Smell**
- Sharp sweat, the kind that comes from bodies moving fast through cold air — the sweat of people doing something they have been waiting to do
- A trace of torch oil, different from lamp oil — the zealots carry their own light

**Touch**
- The food queue crowd closes behind the players as the south zealot moves up, not aggressively but completely: there is nowhere to back into
- The cold air on the back of the neck that says every exit has something in front of it

**Taste**
- The dry metal taste of adrenaline, and underneath it, the same dust — the city always tastes like this; the difference is now you notice

## 4. Pre-Scene Setup

### What Players Know (Coming from the Chancellor's Briefing)

- They have been hired by Chancellor Ostenveld to investigate the Ritual of Eternal Dawn — specifically, whether restoration is possible and what it would cost
- They are heading to the Varenhold Civic Archive in the Spire Quarter, which holds Archmagister Corven's documentation
- They have the name Theron Waide — the Master Archivist — as their contact
- The commission is meant to be low-profile; the Chancellor has not publicized it

### What Players Are Looking For

- Corven's original ritual documents (or access to them via Theron Waide)
- Any physical evidence of what the ritual actually did and whether it can be undone
- The Archive is their next immediate destination — they are crossing through the Lowmark to reach it

### What Players Do NOT Know

- Someone already knows their route
- Edoran exists, knows who they are, and has sent people to intercept them
- The Restorers have any operational capability in the Lowmark at street level
- The note exists; the note contains Edoran's handwriting; Edoran is in the alley watching

---

### What Should Be Visible Before Combat Triggers

**No roll required:**
- The sealed note in Z4's belt pouch — describe it as a rolled document with a dark wax seal, carried in a visible open-top pouch on Z4's belt. Any player who scans the group before the fight starts can see it without a roll.
- The group arrangement itself: four people who are not in the queue, positioned at exits. A DC 10 Perception (passive) notices this feels wrong. DC 12 registers "these are exits."

**DC 15 Perception (active, or passive 15+):**
- A figure 30 ft into the east alley, not moving, watching the junction. Difficult to see because they are standing completely still in shadow and the players are not expecting a watcher. Description if spotted: *"A person in a plain dark coat, hood up, standing with the particular stillness of someone who is not lost. They have been there for some time."* Edoran does not react to being noticed. He watches.

## 5. Cinematic Framing

### GM Opening Sentence (Before Read-Aloud)

Deliver this aloud before reading any variant:

> *"You're one junction from the Archive road. The Lowmark is doing what it always does at this hour — feeding itself, slowly, in the amber half-dark. Let me tell you what you see as you turn the corner."*

Then deliver the appropriate read-aloud variant based on the party's approach.

---

### What NOT to Describe Yet

Do not mention until the relevant moment occurs:

- **Edoran** — not unless a player makes the DC 15 Perception check or actively looks into the east alley. He is the scene's secret. Describing him too early collapses a reveal that pays off in Scene 5.
- **The scripture on the note** — the note is visible, but its contents (*"The willing heart gives freely what the bound heart cannot"*) are only revealed when a player reads it after recovery. Do not describe the inscription in any opening pass.
- **The note-burn mechanic** — do not announce the secondary objective; let players discover it when Z4 starts moving toward the lantern post at the start of round 2. The movement telegraphs intent without explanation.
- **The Enforcer's origin or name** — he will not say who sent him, even if captured. Do not give him identifying details that feel like a faction marker. He is professional and silent and that is the point.
- **The exit route zealots take** — they scatter into the food queue crowd when the Enforcer goes to half HP. Do not describe where they go; let them simply be absorbed by the crowd, which is its own statement about how the Restorers operate in the Lowmark.

---

### The Scene's Core Tension

This is not a fight about survival. The players are almost certainly going to win. The fight is about:

1. **The note** — can they get it before it burns?
2. **Edoran** — is anyone watching the alleys?
3. **The crowd** — how do they handle a public ambush in front of fifteen hungry civilians?

Players who engage all three layers leave this scene with a richer picture than players who only fight. Reward attention over aggression.
