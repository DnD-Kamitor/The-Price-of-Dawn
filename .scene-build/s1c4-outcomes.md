## Sera Relationship States (Three Variants)

### Variant A — Full Engagement

**Conditions:** At least one player asked something personal — the scarf, her faith, what she cooks, how she is. Sera was fully invested in the conversation.

**State:** She knows this player by name. Not as a record — as a person. She will use their name if they meet again. She will, in a later session, tell them something she has not told other people who have asked her the investigation questions without asking anything else.

**In Session 3-5:** She recognizes the party immediately when they return. Before they speak, she pulls out a bench with one foot — same motion as Scene 1. She is ready to have a conversation, not give a briefing. She may, at a relevant moment, volunteer the one thing she has been carrying that she has not said aloud: *"I want the city to deserve it. I'm not always sure it does."* This is her fear made verbal, offered only to someone she has reason to trust.

**At the Inversion Circle:** Her stone activates clearly and without hesitation.

---

### Variant B — Adequate

**Conditions:** Players asked good questions about the investigation. They stayed professional. They were not cold — they simply did not ask about her as a person.

**State:** She knows who they are. She is not warm about them but she is not cold. She has told them what they needed to know. If they return in later sessions, she answers their questions. She does not volunteer more.

**In Session 3-5:** She will recognize the party and provide information accurately. She will not sit with them unless they have a clear purpose. The relationship is functional.

**At the Inversion Circle:** Her stone activates on schedule.

---

### Variant C — Transactional

**Conditions:** Players treated her as an information source. They asked their questions efficiently, received answers, and departed without asking anything that wasn't on the investigation list.

**State:** She answered their questions. She does not particularly remember them as individuals. They are "the Chancellor's investigators" in her mind, not people.

**In Session 3-5:** She answers questions put to her correctly. She does not offer more than asked. If players are in a difficult moment in Session 5 and need something specific from her, she will provide it — but they will have to ask for it directly. It will not be offered.

**At the Inversion Circle:** Her stone activates last.

## Forward Threads to Sessions 2-5

**Thread 1 — The Ashring Inscription (Session 4)**

She has traced the inscription with her fingers. She cannot read it. When players find the translation in Session 4, they will understand that the stones record the names of the ritual's intended function — not the Dawnborn's names, but the *role* each position was designed to fulfill. The inscription at Stone 1 says, in translation: *"The one who stays."* This is not a description of what she does in the ritual. It is a description of what she has done with her life.

The scene lands differently if players have met her first.

**Thread 2 — Tomas Areth (Session 3)**

She says he is *"braver than he knows."*

In Session 3, players will meet Tomas. They will discover that he is a man in active, scholarly, methodical terror about what his consent means — not whether to give it, but what it costs to give it well. He has been writing a journal called the Asymmetry Journal that documents the specific ways in which each Dawnborn's situation is not equivalent to the others.

Sera's read: he is working through the fear with the tools available to him. The tools are rigorous. The work is real. The bravery is in continuing it. She is right. Players who return to this scene in their minds when they meet Tomas will understand both of them better.

**Thread 3 — Fen, the Teenager (Session 4 or later)**

If players specifically asked about the teenager with the ladle, they have a name: Fen. Three weeks at the Dawnhall. Born the same night as the Dawnborn — not a primary anchor, not one of the ten, but something adjacent. Sera noticed immediately and has never asked Fen about it.

This thread only activates if players went looking for it. It will not be surfaced automatically. If players did ask, they have a character who may be relevant to the ritual's edge cases — and a reason to return to the Dawnhall that isn't Sera.
