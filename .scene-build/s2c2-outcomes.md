**The answer, in plain language:**

> THE DAWNBORN ARE THE VESSEL. THE ENERGY LIVES WITHIN THEM.

This sentence exists in the players' possession by the end of this scene — either decoded by them or spoken by Theron in the third-attempt recovery. There is no path through Scene 2 where the players leave without it.

## Theron's Status Post-Scene

He has crossed a threshold. The slips are out of the drawer. He cannot put them back.

Before this scene, Theron was a bystander who kept records. After this scene, he is a participant — someone who has actively introduced players into a situation he has managed silently for eleven years.

He knows this. Players who are watching him will see that he knows this. He does not look relieved. He looks like someone who has handed over something they cannot take back, which is not quite the same as relief.

This matters in Sessions 3–5. Theron's threshold-crossing here is what makes him useful and available as an ally later. He is no longer a neutral party. He has chosen a side, however quietly.

---

## Physical Evidence in Players' Possession

The three slips remain on the table at the end of this scene. Theron does not reclaim them unless players initiate the third-attempt recovery and give them back voluntarily.

**The slips as evidence:** If the Reckoning raids again and searches the players' persons or belongings, these slips are recoverable. Players who take them should be aware that they are carrying the same evidence that Theron kept hidden for eleven years. What they do with that custody is a choice the campaign will notice.

---

## Campaign Hooks Unlocked

**The suppressed prior disclosure**
Theron mentioned telling the previous Chancellor. The players now know there was an attempt to surface this before, approximately 10–12 years ago, that was met with administrative suppression. What happened to the previous Chancellor? What did the current Chancellor inherit, and does he know?

**Slip 3's rewrite (if noticed)**
A player who succeeded on the DC 15 Insight check noticed the slightly different ink in the third slip. Theron corrected an error in his own code. He went back and made sure it could be found. Players who noticed this know something about Theron that he has never said aloud: he has been maintaining the possibility of discovery for years. This is not a secret he protects. It is a secret he does not yet know he revealed.

**The locked drawer**
The drawer is now empty except for a ribbon and a key hook. If players think to ask what else Theron kept there, or what else he has removed: he will not answer directly. He will say the slips were the only thing in the drawer that mattered. He is telling the truth, narrowly.

## Scene 3 Setup

Near the end of this scene's natural rest — after the "Yes" and the open moment and whatever conversation follows — Theron stands. He straightens his pen case. He says:

> "There's one more thing. Upstairs."

He leads them out of the reading room and up the Archive's interior stairs to his private study. The resonance box is visible on the shelf behind his desk before he points to it.
