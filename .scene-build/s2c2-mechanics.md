## Fragment 1 Interaction (Session 1 Carryover)

Characters who decoded any portion of Fragment 1 in Session 1 receive the following benefits in this scene:

**"Living conduit mechanism" from Fragment 1 → "vessel must be willing" in Slip 1**

Any character who encountered the phrase "living conduit mechanism" in Fragment 1 and succeeded on its Arcana check recognizes the conceptual parallel when they read Slip 1. This is not a new check — it is a recall moment. The GM should prompt the player privately or ask them to make a DC 8 History check (trivial — this is a reminder, not a test).

Result: the character gains **advantage on the next Arcana check made in Scene 3** (the resonance box examination). They have begun to triangulate the framework before Theron confirms it.

**"Distributed sympathetic anchors" from Fragment 1 → "anchor holds" in Slip 1**

A character who specifically decoded the anchor notation from Fragment 1 recognizes "anchor holds" as a technical phrase, not a nautical metaphor. This is automatic if the character decoded that portion of Fragment 1.

Result: the character gains **advantage on Investigation DC 12** (identifying the encoding type) because they are pattern-trained from Session 1 work.

## No Time Pressure

This is explicitly a patience puzzle. There is no countdown, no incoming threat, no reason to rush.

The only escalation in this scene is social: Theron reaches for the slips on the third failed attempt. This is not a mechanical penalty — it is a narrative prompt that initiates the recovery sequence. The players can prevent it by holding onto the slips. The slips are on the table; anyone at the table can simply not release them.

**If players ask "is there a time limit?":** There is not. The Archive is quiet. The Reckoning has retreated. The building is theirs for now.

**If players ask if reinforcements are coming:** Theron doesn't know. He says so.

## Skill Substitution

The puzzle is designed around Investigation, but the following substitutions are valid at GM discretion:

| Substitution | Justification |
|---|---|
| Insight instead of Investigation (DC 14) | A character reading Theron's intent rather than the text — they understand *how* he thinks and apply that to find the method. Requires the DC 12 Insight success first. |
| Arcana instead of Investigation (DC 15) | A character approaching this as a notational system rather than a cipher. Higher DC because it's an indirect route. |
| History instead of Investigation (DC 8) | Background knowledge of distributed notation removes the dead-end phase entirely — the character knows the method, needs only to determine N. |

---

## Group Problem-Solving

This puzzle is designed for the table, not a single character. Multiple approaches can combine:

- One character makes the DC 8 History check (distributed notation — it's "every N words")
- Another makes the DC 12 Investigation check (confirms the method, narrows N)
- A third recognizes the Arcana connection that confirms N=3 via "third circuit"

Players may freely share information. Theron watches all of it.

**If players ask Theron to confirm their method without giving him the answer:** He will confirm or deny with a single word. He is not trying to make this harder than he made it. He is trying to make sure they get there.
