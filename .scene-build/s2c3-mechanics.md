## Fragment 2: Partial Cipher Content

Fragment 2 is written in Sali's handwriting on a folded sheet approximately 20cm × 15cm. It is organized in three columns:

**Column 1 — The Symbol:** Each entry shows a notation glyph drawn precisely. These match symbols visible in Corven's technical pages (pages 1-2 of the ritual notes).

**Column 2 — Plain-tongue Equivalent:** Each symbol translated to common language. Not all translations are single words; some are phrase-concepts.

**Column 3 — Contextual Note:** Sali's brief annotation on when/how the symbol is used.

### Symbols Covered by Fragment 2 (GM reference)

| Symbol Class | Plain-tongue | Sali's Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lux-anchor binding glyphs (6 symbols) | "vessel," "hold," "willing," "inversion point," "release condition," "sustained" | "These are the anchor terms. Each one appears once per vessel reference in the notation." |
| Energy distribution notation (4 symbols) | "dispersed," "condensed," "living transfer," "incomplete" | "Corven uses 'incomplete' where others write 'failed.' This is intentional — see his margin notes." |
| Ritual phase markers (3 symbols) | "initiated," "redirected," "pending completion" | "Three phases. Only the first two appear in the Archive records. The third is present only in the original notes." |
| Witness/observer markers (2 symbols) | "confirmed witness," "silent witness" | "The distinction matters. Not everyone present was an authorized witness." |

### What Fragments 1+2 Combined Unlock

Fragment 1 (from Session 1) covered the general notation structure: how symbols are grouped, the reading direction, the numeric modifiers, and the time-reference system.

Fragment 2 covers the specific semantic vocabulary of the energy/anchor/vessel system.

**Combined capability:** Players with both fragments can now decode:
- All vessel and anchor references in Corven's technical notation (pages 1-2)
- The energy distribution diagram on page 1 (which shows ten vessel positions arranged in a specific geometric pattern — the Ashring plaza configuration)
- The "pending completion" phase marker and what it requires
- The difference between "dispersed" (what the public record shows) and "living transfer" (what actually happened)

**What they still cannot decode without Fragment 3:**
- The completion mechanism (what "release" means technically — the method, not just the concept)
- The ritual timeline notation (when the window closes)
- The specific binding notation for each of the ten vessels (which would, in theory, allow identification by birth record cross-referencing)

**Fragment 3 location:** Session 3. Players who have Fragments 1+2 will encounter Fragment 3 in a context that rewards having done the prior work.
