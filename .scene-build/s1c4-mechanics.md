## Social Mechanics

---

### Are There Rolls?

This scene is not gated by dice. Sera has decided to be present with these players before they arrive. No Persuasion check unlocks her honesty. No Intimidation extracts information she is withholding. She is not performing withholding — she is having a conversation, and the conversation goes at the pace of honesty.

The rolls that exist here are **Insight** checks — not to extract information but to understand what is underneath what Sera is saying — and **Perception** checks to notice specific physical details that inform the scene.

**Persuasion:** Not relevant. She will not be moved by argument. She will not be charmed into consent or charity about other Dawnborn's positions. She is warm; she is not manipulable. The warmth and the steadiness are not in tension — they are the same thing.

**Intimidation:** She is a woman who has known for years that she will die so the sun returns. The threat spectrum available to players in this room does not operate on her.

---

### Insight DC Table

All Insight information is available to a player who rolls at any point during the scene. These are not gating rolls — a perceptive player who asks good questions may receive the same information through dialogue.

| DC | What a player notices |
|---|---|
| **10** | She is at peace with something. The specific quality of her composure is not a performance — it is the composure of a person who has been sitting with a hard truth long enough that the truth has stopped disturbing their surface. |
| **13** | She has known for a long time. Not days or weeks — years. The way she receives what players tell her is not the way someone receives new information. It is the way someone receives confirmation. |
| **15** | She is afraid sometimes. Not of death — the fear is more specific than that. It is the fear of dying for something undeserving. The fear is present and managed, like a room she has learned to walk past without entering. |
| **18** | She is performing nothing. There is no gap between the woman in front of them and what she actually is. What players see is exactly what she is. This is rare enough to be remarkable. |

### Perception DC Table

| DC | What a player notices |
|---|---|
| **10** | The yellow scarf on the hook by the door. It is the only yellow object in the room. |
| **13** | The pendant at her collarbone is warm — not body-warm, warm above room temperature — at close range. A player who is within five feet of her and paying attention will notice the slight heat before they consciously register what they are noticing. |
| **15** | She touches the pendant before answering specific questions — only the questions where she is being careful. A DC 15 Perception identifies the pattern. |

### History Checks

| DC | Information |
|---|---|
| **12** | The Dawnhall was a grain storage building before Sera's occupancy. This building was literally food before it was care — same function, different form. The city warden's permit records say "community nutrition program." She did not write "healing centre" because she was not sure yet that it would be one. |

### Medicine Checks

| DC | Information |
|---|---|
| **13** | Most of the people in this room are in late-stage grey sickness. The Dawnhall is providing palliative care as much as treatment — food and warmth to ease the progression, not reverse it. The man coughing in the far corner has, at best, another season. The child leaning against her mother is at an early stage, still reversible if circumstances change. The care here is real and fully informed about what it cannot do. |

### What Players Can Take from the Scene

No physical items. What players take is informational.

| Information | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Dawnborn know what they are | Sera confirms this directly | She found out herself through the same Archive sources |
| Consent is individual and non-transferable | Sera states this as a principle | *"My yes is mine. Theirs is theirs."* |
| Sera has genuinely consented | Observable through Insight and conversation | The difference between performed and genuine acceptance is distinguishable |
| The Dawnhall demonstrates embeddedness | Environmental observation | The Dawnborn are not isolated figures — they are woven into the city's daily life |
| The Ashring stones have a physical effect on Dawnborn | Sera's direct report | *"Like being recognized."* Stone 1 responded to her presence |
| The Ashring inscription exists but is unreadable to her | Sera volunteers this | A thread for Session 4 |
| Tomas Areth is "braver than he knows" | Sera's aside | Players will understand this differently by Session 3 |
| The ritual timeline is real but not immediate | Inferred from Sera's composure | She does not know about the 7-day clock; she treats the matter as serious but not urgent |

### What Sera Will Not Provide

| Information | Reason |
|---|---|
| Whether other Dawnborn have consented | Not hers to share. Principle. |
| Names of Dawnborn who have spoken to the Reckoning | She doesn't know, and would not say if she did |
| The current status of Harran Lecht's operation | She knows the Reckoning is active but not their specific timeline |
| The inversion pathway (all ten freely consent = no deaths) | She does not know about this; it is in the deep Archive material players haven't reached yet |

### The Teenager with the Ladle

If players specifically ask Sera who the teenager is:

> "Fen. Three weeks. Good instincts."

She volunteers nothing more. If players persist:

> "Why do you ask?"

If players have a genuine reason to pursue this — if their character would logically care about who else Sera trusts in the Dawnhall — she tells them Fen was born the night of the great inversion. The same night as the Dawnborn. Not a primary anchor, she says — the ten positions were already filled by the time the energy distributed. But something adjacent. She noticed it immediately. She has never asked Fen about it.

*This is a late-session thread. Do not push it unless players pursue it specifically.*
