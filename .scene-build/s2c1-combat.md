## Initiative Setup

**Who rolls:** All player characters, the Lieutenant (Dex +0), and Soldiers as a group (Dex +1, roll once for all four).

**Surprise:** Players are NOT surprised unless they lingered at the door long enough for Soldier 4 to spot them (DC 12 Stealth to enter without being seen). If seen, combat begins immediately — no surprise round. If unseen, players may take one action before initiative is rolled (observing the room, grabbing Bundle C, calling out to the Lieutenant).

**Opening image (read aloud before initiative):**
> The door handle is warm under your hand. Inside: high shelves on both walls, amber light from the east, and the smell of smoke that shouldn't be here. Three bundles of documents sit on the reading tables, arranged in a line. A man in a grey tabard stands at the far end of the room holding one of them, scanning the first page with the focus of someone confirming rather than reading. Around him: four soldiers, moving with coordination. One of them is already reaching for the bundle nearest the fire.
> *What do you do?*

## Round-by-Round Threat Escalation

If players do not intervene on a given bundle:

| Round | Bundle A | Bundle B | Bundle C | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Start** | East table, uncontested | Center table, uncontested | West table, uncontested | Players enter. Soldier 1 already moving toward Bundle A. |
| **Round 1** | Soldier 1 picks up Bundle A, moves east | Soldier 2 moves toward Bundle B | Soldier 3 moves to intercept any player going for C | Soldier 4 blocks west entrance. Lieutenant watches. |
| **Round 2** | **Soldier 1 throws Bundle A into fire** (if uncontested) | Soldier 2 picks up Bundle B, begins moving east | Soldier 3 reaches Bundle C | If Bundle A is saved, Soldier 1 turns to fight. |
| **Round 3** | Bundle A: LOST (if not saved R1) | **Soldier 2 throws Bundle B into fire** (if uncontested) | Soldier 3 moves Bundle C east | Lieutenant joins combat if players are winning. |
| **Round 4** | — | Bundle B: LOST (if not saved R2) | **Soldier 3 throws Bundle C into fire** (if uncontested) | Morale check if Lt down + 2 soldiers down. |

**GM note:** This table represents worst case. Players will almost certainly disrupt at least one thread. The pressure is intentional — they cannot passively watch and then act.

## Secondary Objective Mechanics

### Contesting a Bundle Carry

When a soldier is carrying a bundle and a player wants to take it:

| Action | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| **Grab from carrier** | Action. Contested Str (Athletics). On success: player holds the bundle. Soldier is not grappled. |
| **Grapple the carrier** | Action. Contested Str (Athletics) vs Str (Athletics) or Dex (Acrobatics). On success: soldier is grappled, bundle drops to floor. |
| **Intercept (cut off path)** | Opportunity attack when soldier moves through threatened space. If attack hits and deals 10+ damage: soldier drops bundle, DM's discretion. |
| **Bundle on floor** | DC 10 Athletics as bonus action to scoop up while moving past. Otherwise requires an action. |

### The Throw-Rather-Than-Lose Rule

If a player has successfully grappled a carrying soldier and the fireplace is within 10 ft:
- Soldier may use their bonus action to throw the bundle into the fire (treat as improvised ranged attack, no to-hit required for the fire — it's a large target).
- Players can use their reaction to catch or deflect: DC 12 Dex save. On success: bundle lands on floor 1d4 × 5 ft away from the fire. On failure: bundle enters fire.

### What Counts as "Secured"

A bundle is secured when:
- A player is holding it and has moved at least 20 ft from the fireplace, **or**
- A player has placed it outside the room (through the west entrance or window), **or**
- The bundle has been moved to the alcove area (near Theron's door) — soldiers will not prioritize re-taking it from that position after Round 2

A bundle is NOT secured while:
- On the floor anywhere in the room (soldiers can scoop it as a bonus action)
- Held by a player who is grappled, incapacitated, or restrained

## Lieutenant Capture Mechanics

**When he surrenders vs. fights to escape:**

| Condition | Lieutenant's Response |
|---|---|
| Reduced to 25 HP or below | Continues fighting but stops directing soldiers. Focused on his own survival now. |
| Reduced to 0 HP | Incapacitated. Can be stabilized and interrogated. |
| Grappled while above 25 HP | Uses action to break grapple (Str +3). Will not surrender yet. |
| Grappled while below 25 HP | DC 14 Persuasion or Intimidation to get him to stop fighting. He is not a martyr. |
| Bundles all secured or all burned | If both conditions are met, he disengages and attempts to leave. He will not waste casualties on a completed or failed operation. |
| Asked a genuine question mid-combat | He pauses for one exchange. This is not a trick — he genuinely considers dialogue more efficient than injury. Will not surrender but will talk briefly. |

**Escape attempt:** If incapacitation is imminent and bundles are already gone, he attempts to Disengage toward the east window. He does not have a key — he will smash the window (object interaction). This is his last option. He would rather be taken than have his body found.

## Soldier Morale

| State | Behavior |
|---|---|
| All four up, Lt directing | Focused. Prioritize bundles. Will not break from task for any provocation short of direct attack. |
| 1 soldier down | Slight hesitation. Continue mission. No morale check yet. |
| 2 soldiers down | **Morale check required if Lt is also down.** DC 12 Wisdom save (roll for remaining soldiers as a group). On fail: withdraw. On success: continue. |
| Lt incapacitated + 2 soldiers down | Automatic withdraw. Remaining soldiers disengage. Not suicidal. |
| Lt incapacitated + all bundles gone | Soldiers leave immediately. The mission is over. |

## Combat End Conditions

### Path 1: Lieutenant Captured / Incapacitated
- Soldiers withdraw (morale breaks per table above)
- Scene ends with Lieutenant in players' custody
- Proceed to interrogation (see `s2c1-outcomes.md`)
- Theron emerges from alcove within 1d4 minutes

### Path 2: Two Bundles Secured
- Remaining soldiers have no mission to complete with fewer than three bundles
- Lieutenant signals withdrawal: *"Fall back. Secondary site."*
- He disengages. Soldiers disengage. They leave in good order.
- Scene ends. Lieutenant is gone — players may pursue (he exits to a waiting contact outside; pursuit is a chase, not a combat, and is outside scope of this scene)
- Theron emerges from alcove within 1d4 minutes

### What Does Not End the Scene
- Killing all four soldiers (Lieutenant continues attempting to burn remaining bundles alone)
- Taking Bundle C only (Soldier 1 may still burn Bundle A before players can reach him)
- Incapacitating the Lieutenant without securing bundles (soldiers continue per priority table, Lt recovers unless stabilized)
