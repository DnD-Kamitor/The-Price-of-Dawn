## The Civic Archive Reading Room

**Dimensions:** ~60 ft (east–west) × 40 ft (north–south). High vaulted ceiling, ~20 ft at apex.

**When players arrive:** The west entrance door is ajar. The handle is still warm. A thin draft moves through the room from the open window in the west wall — it carries the first edge of smoke from the fireplace already burning at the east end.

## Environmental Actions

These are available to any player or NPC who spends the appropriate action:

| Action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Topple a bookshelf** | Action + DC 13 Athletics | Creates difficult terrain 10 ft wide. All creatures in path make DC 12 Dex save or take 2d6 bludgeoning and are knocked prone. |
| **Seal the fireplace flue** | Action + DC 14 Athletics (reach flue chain) | Smoke begins filling east end. Creatures within 20 ft of fireplace make DC 10 Con save each round or are poisoned until they leave the area. |
| **Use the window draft** | Bonus action + DC 10 Athletics | Throw a lightweight object or loose page through the window. Not enough for a full bundle. |
| **Douse the fire (water jug)** | Action | A ceramic water jug sits on a side table near the alcove (DC 12 Perception to spot). Thrown on fire: extinguishes it for 1d4 rounds, then it rekindles. Buying time only. |
| **Block the east table** | Action (shove table) | Shoving the east table DC 12 Athletics — creates 5 ft barrier between Soldier 1 and the fireplace. Soldier must use action to move around it. |
| **Grab a bundle from a carrying soldier** | Action + contested Str (Athletics) | See `s2c1-mechanics.md` for full rules. |

## Round-by-Round Soldier Priority

If players do not intervene:

| Round | What Happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Soldier 1 picks up Bundle A and begins moving east toward fireplace. Soldier 2 moves toward Bundle B. Lieutenant signals to proceed. |
| 2 | Soldier 1 throws Bundle A into fire (irreversible). Soldier 2 picks up Bundle B. Soldier 3 moves toward Bundle C. Soldier 4 blocks players. |
| 3 | Soldier 2 throws Bundle B into fire. Soldier 3 reaches Bundle C. |
| 4 | Soldier 3 throws Bundle C. Scene ends: all three bundles destroyed. |

**The math is designed to pressure:** players must split attention immediately or lose Bundle A in Round 2. They cannot ignore the objective and fight cleanly.

---

## Atmosphere Notes

- The fire is already going. There is no dramatic moment where someone lights it — it is simply burning when players arrive. This is deliberate: the operation is already in motion.
- The draft from the west window creates a faint flutter in the loose papers on the reading tables. Players may notice one page slide slightly toward Bundle C's edge.
- The smell arrives before anything else: old paper, leather, woodsmoke. The taste of smoke catches the back of the throat within one round of entering.
- The sound of folios being pulled from shelves has a specific rhythm: not ransacking, not searching — *selecting*. Someone made a list.
