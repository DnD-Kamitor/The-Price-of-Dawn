# S3C1 — Senna Kard & Davan Crel: NPC Profiles

<details><summary>👤 Senna Kard — Full OGAS Profile</summary>

**Occupation:** Dock worker, crane-crew lead, Ashring North wharf
**Age:** Thirty-four
**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, work-calloused hands. Hair pulled back without ceremony. She is wearing her good coat — the one she saves — because she decided this mattered enough. It is slightly too large; it was her husband's.

**O — Objective:** Get someone to acknowledge that the city's inaction is killing her daughters. Not "acknowledge your pain." Acknowledge the specific thing: that people are dying, that children are sick, and that the Dawnhalls have spent forty years promising a solution and haven't delivered one.

**G — Goal (scene-level):** Hold this line in front of this building until someone inside comes down and faces the crowd. She has been here since before the lanterns were trimmed. She will stay until dark.

**A — Attitude:** Furious and exhausted and precise. She does not raise her voice. She has already done the raising-voice part. What is left is persistence.

**S — Secret:** She doesn't believe the protest will work. She's here because she cannot stay home and do nothing while Nia coughs through the night. The protest is the thing she could think of to do. She knows it isn't enough. She is doing it anyway.

---

**Want:** Someone to say her daughters' names and mean it. Not as a rhetorical device. Not as proof of sympathy. Just: to know that Nia is nine and Brek is six and both of them are showing early symptoms, and to treat that as a fact that matters, not a fact that makes you sad.

**Fear:** That the Dawnborn's deaths won't save them anyway. That the city will spend ten lives and her daughters will still be dying in the dark. She has not said this out loud to anyone. It is the thought she has at 3am that she does not permit herself during the day.

**Lie:** "I'm here for politics." She says this if pressed about why she's here. She means: *I am not here for politics at all, I am here because Nia is nine and Brek is six and I have run out of other options.*

---

**The Placard:**

*Front (visible from the south approach):*
> WE GAVE THE DAWNHALLS EVERYTHING
> WHAT DID IT GIVE US BACK

*Reverse (charcoal, smaller, Senna's own hand — visible DC 10 from 10ft, automatic if players approach her):*
> My daughters' names are Nia and Brek.
> They are nine and six.
> I am not here for politics.

---

**Her Crack — What Reaches Her:**

Two things stop Senna arguing:

1. **A player names her daughters specifically.** Not "your family" — Nia and Brek. By name. The names are on the back of her placard. Anyone who bothered to read it knows them. Anyone who uses them is telling her: *I looked. I read what you wrote. I know who you are here for.*

2. **A player acknowledges the specific fear she hasn't said aloud:** that the ritual might not work even if the Dawnborn die. That the city might sacrifice ten lives and her daughters might still be in the grey sickness's progression. This is Senna's 3am thought. If a player says it to her face, she goes very still. She has never heard anyone else say it. She didn't know other people knew it was possible.

Either crack is enough. Both together produce the shift: *"If they die for this, I need to know they chose it."* She says this once. She does not elaborate.

---

**Dialogue Lines — Social Path:**

*On first contact (players approach from south):*
> "You're not here for chandler's supplies." *(beat)* "I don't care why you're here. Just don't get between me and that door."

*If players say they're looking for the researcher upstairs:*
> "Good. Tell her to come down. Tell her thirty people have been standing in this alley since before the morning bell and not one of us has had an answer."

*If players say they can't discuss it publicly:*
> "Then discuss it in front of witnesses. Those are the same thing right now."

*If players try generic sympathy ("we understand your frustration"):*
> "I'm not frustrated. I'm here. There's a difference." *(She doesn't look away.)* "Frustrated is what I was six months ago."

*If players mention the Dawnborn:*
> "I know what they carry. I've been in the Dawnhalls three times this year. Three times. They told me the ritual was being studied. They told me the situation was being monitored." *(pause)* "Nia's been coughing since the summer."

*If players say the Dawnborn haven't chosen yet:*
> "Then make them choose. Or give me a reason why they shouldn't have to."

*If players say they're trying to find another way:*
> "Another way." *(She looks at them for a long moment.)* "What does that mean?"

*If players explain the Transfer Method:*
> "You're telling me someone upstairs has been working on a way to save them — all of them — and the Dawnhalls have known about it for three years." *(very quiet)* "Three years."

*If a player names Nia and Brek specifically:*
> She stops.
> Not dramatic — she just stops talking.
> A moment.
> Then: "You read the back." *(not a question)* "Most people don't bother."

*If a player acknowledges the ritual might not work even if the Dawnborn die:*
> She looks at them with something that isn't anger.
> "Say that again."
> *(If they repeat it:)* "I've been thinking that for eight months and I haven't said it to anyone because — " *(She stops.)* "I didn't think other people were allowed to think it."

*The shift — after crack is reached:*
> "If they die for this, I need to know they chose it."
> *(Silence. She doesn't move from where she's standing. But she is no longer blocking.)*

*If players ask what she'll do now:*
> "Go home. Make sure Nia ate something." *(beat)* "Come back if I have to."

*If players ask her to trust them:*
> "I'm not going anywhere. You don't need my trust. You need to go upstairs and find out if what's up there is real." *(pause)* "If it is — come back and tell me."

*If players ask about her husband:*
> "Torren. Grey sickness. Two years ago March." *(She says this the way people say things they have said many times.)* "He was on the same crane crew I'm on now. They gave me his shift." *(She doesn't elaborate on whether that was kind or cruel.)*

*If players try to move her by force:*
> She steps back. She does not fight. She watches.
> *(to the crowd, flat):* "They're moving us."
> *(The crowd tightens.)*

---

**History:** Dock worker, crane-crew lead, Ashring North wharf. Born and raised Varenhold. Married Torren Kard, dock worker, same crew. He died of grey sickness two years ago, March. Daughters Nia (9) and Brek (6) are showing early symptoms — fatigue, pallor, occasional joint pain. Standard grey sickness onset. Senna has taken them to the Dawnhall healers three times and received the same answer each time: the situation is being addressed.

She has been organizing this crowd for two weeks, quietly, without speeches. She is not a protest organizer by nature. She is a logistics person. She figured out who to talk to, what time to arrive, and what to bring.

<details><summary>👤 Davan Crel — Profile & Dialogue</summary>

**Occupation:** Aide to Warden Keseph Vyne, Amber Compact
**Age:** Late twenties
**Appearance:** Neat, unremarkable. Dark coat, good boots, nothing distinctive. He looks like a clerk on a half-day's leave. He is sitting at the café's window table with a cup of tea that went cold twenty minutes ago. He has not touched it since.

**O — Objective:** Observe who approaches the building. Log any players' involvement. Confirm whether Isolde is still in residence.

**G — Goal (session-level):** He already sent Isolde's location to Harran Lecht within hours of the players' Session 2 activities. He is here now not to interfere but to collect additional intelligence. If players make it upstairs, Vyne will want to know.

**A — Attitude:** Professionally pleasant. He will not volunteer anything. He will answer questions in ways that are technically true and strategically misleading. He is not frightened of players — he's confident he has already done the thing that matters.

**S — Secret:** He sent the report. Harran Lecht's soldiers are already coming. Whether players detain him, threaten him, or ignore him, the raid in S3C5 happens. The message is gone.

**Tell:** Cold tea. He has been sitting at that table for as long as the crowd has been in the alley. He ordered tea when he arrived. He has not touched it. He is watching the alley entrance with the careful attention of someone who is not supposed to be watching the alley entrance.

---

**Dialogue Lines — If Players Approach Him:**

*On initial contact:*
> "Quite a gathering out there." *(pleasantly)* "I've been trying to decide if I should find another café."

*If players ask what he's watching:*
> "I'm not watching anything. I'm waiting for a colleague who is — " *(checks, unhurried)* " — apparently delayed."

*If players ask his name:*
> "Davan. I work for the civic archive." *(This is not technically false. The Amber Compact has archive offices.)*

*If players press with Insight (DC 14):*
> He doesn't change expression. He has done this before.
> "I understand the concern. It's an unsettling crowd. I think it will disperse on its own."

*If players accuse him of surveillance:*
> "That's — " *(small pause, calculated)* "— quite a conclusion. I've ordered tea. The tea is there." *(He gestures to the cold cup.)*

*If a player points out the tea is cold:*
> He looks at the cup.
> A beat too long.
> "I forgot about it." *(He picks it up and drinks. It is clearly cold. He does not react.)*

---

**What He Has Already Done:**

During Session 2, players' investigation of the Amber Compact's suppression records led to enough identifying information about Isolde's workroom location. Davan — who has been following the investigation at Vyne's direction — sent that location to Harran Lecht's command structure within hours.

The raid in Session 3, Scene 5 happens regardless of what players do to Davan in this scene. If they detain him: someone else carries the message, or it was already carried. If they threaten him: he admits to nothing and the message is still sent. If they kill him (extreme): the message is still gone. The information was already in motion before players arrived at Lowmark Alley.

::: {.prop-alt-ink}
**GM:** Davan is this scene's invisible consequence. Players who notice him and act on the information will adjust their operational security in Sessions 3-5. Players who don't notice him will learn why it matters when Harran's soldiers arrive in S3C5.

Do not make him obviously suspicious. Cold tea is the tell. If no player notices the cold tea, that's fine — it's there for players who look. The DC 13 Perception check is to spot him as someone who doesn't fit the casual-bystander profile; the cold tea is the follow-up detail for players who investigate further.

He is not the scene's antagonist. He's the scene's cost, already paid.
