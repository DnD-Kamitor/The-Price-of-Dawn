## Reckoning Lieutenant — "Carrow"

*Use Knight stats, MM p.347. HP 52. AC 18 (plate). Speed 30 ft.*
*Attacks: Greatsword +5 to hit, 2d6+3 slashing. Leadership action (recharge 5-6): allies within 30 ft add 1d4 to one attack roll or saving throw.*

### Physical Description

Thirties. Short-cropped dark hair going grey at the temples. Reckoning tabard — grey and black, the sun-circle sigil worn over the breast — worn under an open coat, as if he pulled it on in transit. He moves with deliberate economy: no wasted steps, no theatrical positioning. When he picks up a bundle to check it, he holds it at reading distance and scans the first page before setting it back down. He is not skimming. He is confirming.

He does not look up when the players enter.

### OGAS

| | |
|---|---|
| **Occupation** | Reckoning field officer, 8 years service. Career built on operations requiring precision and discretion. Used to acting inside bureaucratic structures where failure means the structure fails, not just the man. |
| **Goal** | Burn all three bundles before any investigator or outside party can remove them. This specific task. Nothing more. He has a narrow mandate and he intends to execute it cleanly. |
| **Attitude** | Professional, not cruel. The burning is not theatrical. It is administrative. He would prefer the players not be here. He would prefer this to have happened yesterday before anyone thought to come back. |
| **Secret** | He has already read the documents. All three bundles. Three weeks ago, when the burn order first came down, he pulled them to confirm the mandate. He read them in full. He burned his own copy of his notes afterward. He has not told anyone in his chain of command that he read them. He is not sure why. He tells himself it was operational prudence. He has not examined that claim closely. |

### Want / Fear / Lie

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | To complete this task cleanly and leave. No casualties, no witnesses who can name him, no complications. |
| **Fear** | That this task is already too late. Players being here means someone sent them. That means there are already copies he doesn't know about, or that the documents have already been read by someone with the ability to act on them. The burn order may be theater at this point. He suspected this three days ago and has not said so. |
| **Lie** | That he is simply following orders. He knows what he's burning. He made a choice to burn it anyway. He has not called that choice by its real name. |

### Dialogue

**Opening line (if players call out before initiative):**
> "This is a restricted operation. Leave now and there's no record of you being here."
> He does not look up from the bundle he is checking. One hand rests near his sword. He is not drawing it.

**During combat (if engaged directly):**
> "You're making this harder than it needs to be."
> *(After losing first soldier)* "Hold the door. Let them commit."

**If a bundle goes into the fire:**
> *(Flat, without satisfaction)* "That's one."

**If captured / incapacitated:**
> *(After a beat of silence)* "You found what you found. So did we. The difference is we acted on it."
> He will not give Harran's location. He will, if pressed with a DC 14 Insight-supported Persuasion or if the players demonstrate they already know what the documents say, confirm:
> *"The Commander already knows what's in them. This wasn't about discovery. This was about control."*

### Interrogation — What He Gives Up

## Reckoning Soldiers (×4)

*Use Veteran stats, MM p.350. HP 58 each. AC 17 (splint). Speed 30 ft.*
*Attacks: Multiattack (longsword ×2, +5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing). Shortsword as offhand.*

### Physical Descriptions (use as needed)

- **Soldier 1 (east, near Bundle A):** Stocky, mid-forties, grey at the edges. Moves without hesitation. He has been told what to do and is doing it. Will not make eye contact with players.
- **Soldier 2 (center, near Bundle B):** Younger, maybe late twenties. Watchful. Scans the room when players enter — he clocked the door before the others did.
- **Soldier 3 (south shelves):** Tall, lean, missing the tip of his left index finger. Positioning himself to intercept players moving toward Bundle C.
- **Soldier 4 (near players / blocking):** Broadest of the four. Positioned deliberately between the west entrance and the tables. He is the door guard. He expected to turn away a librarian, not investigators.

### Tactical Behavior

Soldiers follow the Lieutenant's operational priority:

1. **First priority: move bundles toward the fireplace.** If a soldier is carrying a bundle, they will not drop it to fight — they will take a hit rather than lose the bundle.
2. **Second priority: block players from reaching the carrying soldier.** Soldier 4 and Soldier 3 are designated blockers.
3. **Throw rather than fight over it.** If a player grapples a carrying soldier and the fireplace is within 10 ft, the soldier will use their bonus action to *throw* the bundle into the fire rather than lose it in a prolonged contest. (See `s2c1-mechanics.md` for contested throw rules.)
4. **Will not fight to the death.** If the Lieutenant is incapacitated and 2+ soldiers are down, remaining soldiers withdraw in good order toward the east window (locked — DC 12 to open — they smash it if necessary). They do not surrender; they disengage.

### What They Know

Very little. They were told:
- Report to the Civic Archive at this hour.
- Burn three specific document bundles.
- Do not let anyone remove them.
- The Lieutenant is in command.

They do not know what is in the documents. They have not read them. If players try to tell them what the documents contain, they listen but do not react. They have heard stranger things claimed in field operations. It does not change their orders.

### Morale Break Threshold

Soldiers break when:
- Lieutenant is incapacitated **and**
- 2 or more soldiers are reduced to 0 HP

Remaining soldiers disengage (use Disengage action) and attempt to exit. They are not suicidal. This is a document-burning operation, not a last stand.
