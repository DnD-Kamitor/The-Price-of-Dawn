The Price of Dawn — d100 Character & Party Grounding Table
Table Icebreakers & RP Anchors · Roll 1d100 before the session, during rests, or at campfires
I. Campfire, Habits & Rest (01–10)
01. [Night Watch] When you take your turn on watch while the others sleep, how do you keep yourself awake in the cold silence?
02. [Sleep Quirks] Are you a silent sleeper who barely moves, or do you snore loudly, toss and turn, or mumble in your sleep?
03. [Waking Up] What does your character look and act like in the first ten minutes after waking up? Grumpy, energetic, or dead to the world?
04. [Campfire Habit] Around a fire or stove, what is your habitual idle task: whittling wood, polishing steel, tending the coals, or cleaning boots?
05. [Blankets] In the freezing chill of the city, do you sleep wrapped in every blanket you own, or are you stubbornly tough against the cold?
06. [Personal Trinket] What small, seemingly useless trinket or token do you keep in your pocket that you habitually fiddle with when thinking?
07. [Writing & Notes] Do you keep a written journal, tally marks on your belt, a sketchbook, or do you keep everything locked in your head?
08. [Morning Brew] What is your morning beverage of choice before you can face the day: strong black tea, hot chicory, spiced ale, or boiled water?
09. [Nightmares] When you have a bad dream, is it about a past failure, a terrifying monster you faced, or being left behind in the dark?
10. [Hygiene] How meticulous are you about personal cleanliness on the road: fresh-scented and groomed, or proudly grimy and smelling of campfire?
II. The Party & Companion Bonds (11–20)
11. [First Impression] Think of another player's character. What was your very first impression of them, and how has that opinion changed since?
12. [Combat Trust] Which companion would you want guarding your back if you were cornered in a dark alley by five armed enemies?
13. [Pet Peeve] What is one minor, harmless habit of another party member that secretly drives you crazy on long journeys?
14. [Secret Admiration] Which companion’s fighting style, magic, or silver tongue do you secretly find the most impressive to watch?
15. [The Comforter] When you are visibly shaken or exhausted after a brutal fight, which companion do you feel most comfortable talking to?
16. [Inside Joke] What is a funny mishap, ridiculous failure, or running joke the party shares that always brings a smirk to your face?
17. [Holding Back] Is there something personal about your history or family that you have deliberately avoided telling the rest of the party?
18. [Disagreement] Which companion do you find yourself arguing with most often, even though you still respect their presence in the group?
19. [A Life Owed] Think of a time a companion saved your life or pulled you out of trouble. Have you ever properly thanked them for it?
20. [Party Role] What do you see as your unofficial role in this group: the protector, the voice of reason, the comic relief, or the wildcard?
III. Combat, Scars & Survival (21–30)
21. [First Instinct] When weapons are drawn and initiative is rolled, what is your immediate instinct: charge forward, seek cover, or size up the foe?
22. [Physical Scar] What physical scar or mark on your body has the most painful story behind it, and do you show it off or keep it hidden?
23. [Closest Call] What was the absolute closest you ever came to dying in battle, and what thought flashed through your head in that moment?
24. [Favored Weapon] What weapon, spell, or item in your arsenal feels like an extension of your own body when you wield it?
25. [Battle Cry] Do you fight with furious shouts, cold grim silence, nervous jokes, or muttered prayers under your breath?
26. [Aftermath] Right after a deadly combat ends and blood cools, what is the very first thing you do: check your wounds, clean your blade, or check on allies?
27. [Mercy] When an enemy drops their weapon and begs for their life, are you inclined to show mercy, or do you finish what was started?
28. [Adrenaline] Does the thrill of combat excite you and make you feel alive, or is fighting a grim, exhausting chore you endure to survive?
29. [A Lost Foe] Is there an enemy you killed whose face still haunts your memories, or do you sleep soundly regardless of body count?
30. [Signature Trick] What is your signature flourish, tactical trick, or stylistic move that your companions have seen you pull off in fights?
IV. Food, Drink & City Taverns (31–40)
31. [Go-To Tavern] When you arrive in a new district, what kind of tavern do you look for: a loud, bustling taproom, a quiet inn, or a shady dive?
32. [Favorite Drink] What is your character's drink of choice when unwinding: dark bitter stout, sweet mead, burning spirits, or plain cider?
33. [Worst Road Meal] What is the most disgusting, burnt, or questionable meal you’ve choked down on the road just to keep from starving?
34. [The Cook] Can your character actually cook decent food, or would the party actively mutiny if you were put in charge of the stew pot?
35. [Comfort Food] What simple, hearty dish reminds you of home and warmth whenever you are cold, battered, and homesick?
36. [A Grand Feast] If someone gave you a bag of gold and said 'eat like royalty tonight,' what extravagant meal would you order?
37. [Tavern Behavior] After having three strong drinks, what kind of drunk are you: loud and friendly, brooding and quiet, emotional, or looking for a fight?
38. [Gambling] Do you enjoy playing dice or card games in smoky taverns, or do you know better than to gamble your hard-earned copper?
39. [Ration Sharing] If travel rations were cut in half due to snow or lockdown, would you quietly share your portion, or protect your share?
40. [A Toast] When raising a tankard with your companions after surviving another day, what is your standard, heartfelt toast?
V. Cold, Shadows & Street Life (41–50)
41. [The Cold] How does your character cope with the bitter city chill: heavy woolen cloaks, thick furs, layers of leather, or toughing it out?
42. [Walking in Darkness] In a city wrapped in eternal twilight, do you feel comfortable moving through dark alleys, or do you always seek lantern light?
43. [City Watch] When you hear heavy boots of a guard patrol stomping down the street, is your first instinct to nod casually, or step into an alley?
44. [Street Smart] What street trick or survival rule—like hiding coin in your boot or watching rooftops—has kept you safe in rough quarters?
45. [The Scent of the City] What smell of Varenhold do you notice most when stepping outdoors: chimney soot, muddy canals, wet wool, or roasting meat?
46. [A Narrow Escape] Have you ever had to sprint for your life through winding cobblestone alleys or over slick rooftops? Who was chasing you?
47. [Street Urchins] When a ragged kid tries to pick your pocket or begs for scraps, do you slap their hand away, give them coin, or offer advice?
48. [Slick Cobbles] Have you ever taken an embarrassing spill on icy cobblestones or slippery canal docks? Did anyone see you do it?
49. [A Quiet Corner] Where in the crowded, noisy city do you go when you need an hour of total peace and quiet to gather your thoughts?
50. [Bridges & Water] How do you feel about the dark, murky canal waters: completely unfazed, or secretly uncomfortable around deep, cold water?
VI. Past, Family & Mentors (51–60)
51. [The Mentor] Who taught you your most important skill or trade, and what is one memorable piece of advice they drilled into your head?
52. [Family Legacy] What did your family or guardians think of your choice to live the dangerous life of an adventurer?
53. [Childhood Best Friend] Who was your closest friend when you were growing up, and do you have any idea what became of them?
54. [A Prized Keepsake] What physical item from your past do you still carry with you everywhere, and why does it mean so much to you?
55. [Childhood Mischief] What was the stupidest, most daring piece of trouble you got into as a kid that almost got you caught?
56. [The Departure] Think back to the day you left home or started wandering. What was the last thing you saw or heard as you walked away?
57. [Unfinished Business] Is there someone from your past that you desperately need to see again, whether to reconcile, apologize, or settle a score?
58. [An Early Trade] Before taking up arms or spellcraft, did you have an ordinary job or apprenticeship: blacksmith, courier, farmer, scribe?
59. [A Parent's Voice] When your conscience speaks to you in a difficult moment, does it sound like your own voice, or the voice of a parent or teacher?
60. [Homesickness] What is one specific thing about your hometown or early life that you miss far more than you ever thought you would?
VII. Secrets, Flaws & Weaknesses (61–70)
61. [The Bad Habit] What is a minor vice or bad habit your character indulges in: smoking a pipe, biting nails, cursing loudly, or hoarding shiny junk?
62. [The Lie] What is a lie you told a companion or stranger that they completely believed, and do you feel guilty about it?
63. [Temptation] What temptation is hardest for you to resist: rare magic, a mountain of gold, good wine, a thrilling challenge, or revenge?
64. [A Deep Regret] If you had the power to turn back time and undo one single mistake you made in your past, what moment would you change?
65. [Blind Spot] What is a personal flaw that you know you have—stubbornness, arrogance, quick temper, or paranoia—that gets you into trouble?
66. [Secret Fear] Beyond monsters and dying, what is your character secretly terrified of: being abandoned, going blind, losing control, or poverty?
67. [Overstepping] Have you ever pushed someone too far in an argument or fight and immediately realized you crossed a line?
68. [A Broken Promise] Is there a promise you made to someone that you broke, and does the memory of that broken vow still bother your sleep?
69. [Vanity] What small aspect of your appearance or gear are you secretly vain about: your hair, your cloak, your boots, or your weapon's sheen?
70. [Vulnerability] What does your character look like when they are completely emotionally overwhelmed? Do you shut down, cry, or lash out in anger?
VIII. Faith, Luck & Superstitions (71–80)
71. [A Quiet Prayer] Do you pray to a specific deity, whisper to general gods of luck and travel, or rely purely on your own two hands?
72. [Good Luck Charm] Do you carry a lucky coin, a worn feather, a knotted cord, or a carved token that you believe keeps bad luck away?
73. [Pre-Battle Ritual] What small superstition do you observe before kicking in a door or entering a dangerous crypt: tapping iron, kissing a token, checking boots?
74. [Omen] What everyday occurrence do you consider a sign of bad luck: a raven landing near you, an overturned salt cellar, or a flickering lamp?
75. [Fate vs Choice] Do you believe your destiny has already been written by the gods, or that every person carves their own path with their choices?
76. [Holy Places] When you step into a temple or sacred shrine, do you feel a sense of peaceful reverence, or an awkward desire to leave quickly?
77. [A Blessing Given] Have you ever offered an honest prayer or blessing for someone else's safety, and who was it for?
78. [Miracles] Have you ever witnessed something so miraculous or inexplicable that it fundamentally shook what you believe about the world?
79. [Cynicism] When a holy priest or preacher promises that 'the gods have a plan for you,' do you find comfort in that, or roll your eyes?
80. [The Final Rest] When your adventuring days are finally over, where in the world would you want your bones to be laid to rest?
IX. Humor, Quirks & Camp Life (81–90)
81. [Weird Backpack Item] What is an unusual, sentimental, or totally non-combat item you stubbornly carry in your backpack at all times?
82. [A Genuine Laugh] When was the last time something was so funny that you laughed until your ribs hurt, and who was there with you?
83. [A Boast] What is an outrageous boast or exaggerated story about yourself that you like to tell around a tavern table?
84. [Camp Duty] Which camp chore do you secretly hate doing the most: gathering firewood, skinning game, washing pots, or standing first watch?
85. [Nicknames] Does your character have an embarrassing childhood nickname, or an alias you used when you got into trouble?
86. [Music & Song] Can your character sing, hum a decent tune, or play an instrument, or are you aggressively tone-deaf?
87. [Animals] How do you react to street dogs, stray cats, or horses: do you immediately try to pet them, or ignore them completely?
88. [Gear Maintenance] Are you obsessive about sharpening your weapons and oiling your leather every evening, or do you clean gear only when forced?
89. [A Peculiar Quirk] What is an odd, unique habit or mannerism of yours: cracking knuckles, speaking with hands, pacing back and forth?
90. [Lifting Spirits] When the party is battered, freezing, and miserable, what is your way of trying to cut the tension or raise morale?
X. The Climax, Legacy & Tomorrow (91–100)
91. [Why You Fight] When you are bruised, freezing, and facing terrible odds today, what core belief or person is driving you to keep standing?
92. [If Tomorrow Comes] If the party succeeds and you survive this final crisis, what is the very first thing you want to do with your newfound freedom?
93. [The Dream Life] Imagine a peaceful life without blood, monsters, and fear. What trade would you practice, and what would your home look like?
94. [Last Words] If your character falls in battle today, what is one final message or confession you would want passed to your companions?
95. [What You'll Miss] When this adventure is over and the party eventually goes their separate ways, what will you miss most about travelling together?
96. [The Line You Won't Cross] No matter how desperate the stakes get in the final hour, what is one line you swear your character will never cross?
97. [A Promise Made] What solemn promise have you made to a companion, a friend, or yourself that you intend to fulfill before the sun rises?
98. [How You Want to Be Remembered] Fifty years from now, when bards or old soldiers speak your name in taverns, what do you hope they say about you?
99. [Facing the Climax] Looking at your companions right now before walking into the final confrontation, what do you feel: dread, calm resolve, or fierce pride?
100. [The Price of Dawn] Every victory has a cost—blood, sweat, sacrifice, or lost innocence. Looking back on the whole journey, was it worth the price?
