The Price of Dawn — d100 Twilight Character Grounding Table
Varenhold Grounding · Roll 1d100 for memories, sensory anchors & moral weight
I. Light, Twilight & The Sky (01–10)
01. [The Sun] Do you personally remember the real sun before the Twilight, or has daylight only ever been a myth and memory told by elders?
02. [The Sky] When you look up at Varenhold's perpetual slate-grey overcast, do you find the unchanging ceiling suffocating, or strangely comforting?
03. [Amber Glow] The city runs on warm amber streetlamps and lanterns. Do you prefer lingering in the warm pools of lamplight, or moving unseen in the shadows?
04. [First Shadow] Under amber light, shadows are sharp and amber-edged. What is your earliest memory of being startled or fascinated by your own shadow?
05. [Colors] Without true sunlight, colors in Varenhold are muted greys, browns, and ambers. What rare, vivid color are you always drawn to when you spot it?
06. [The Lamp] What kind of light source do you personally rely on when travelling through the city: a brass amber lantern, a smoky tallow torch, or magical light?
07. [Blind Spot] Have you ever stared too long into an amber furnace or flare until your eyes hurt? What did you think you saw dancing in the heat?
08. [Nightfall] In a city where it is always dusk, how does your body know when it is time to sleep when bells fail to ring?
09. [Pre-Dawn Lore] What is the most poetic or absurd thing you have heard an elder claim the sun was capable of doing before the sky died?
10. [A Glimpse] If you could see the sun break through the clouds for just sixty seconds, where in the city would you want to be standing to witness it?
II. The Cold, Hearth & Warmth (11–20)
11. [The Chill] Where does the damp cold of Varenhold settle into your body first: your fingers, your boots, your chest, or an old ache in your bones?
12. [Layers] Describe the layers of clothing, cloaks, furs, or wraps your character bundles into daily just to keep the biting frost at bay.
13. [Comfort Food] When you are freezing and exhausted, what hot meal or brew restores your spirit: chicory tea, thick mushroom pottage, or spiced tallow bread?
14. [The Hearth] Do you sleep better beside a crackling hearthfire with light flickering on the walls, or bundled alone in pitch darkness?
15. [Frozen Water] Have you ever slipped or broken through the crust of a frozen canal? What lesson about Varenhold's winter did that close call teach you?
16. [Warmth of Others] Are you someone who readily shares your fire and blankets with shivering strangers, or do you guard your warmth jealously?
17. [Breath] Everyone's breath plumed white in the air. When you speak to someone in the cold, do you watch their eyes, or watch their pluming breath?
18. [Fingers] Your hands must be nimble for your craft, weapon, or spells. How do you keep your fingers warm enough to act when danger strikes?
19. [The Deep Freeze] What was the harshest, most desperate winter night you ever endured, and how did you survive until the morning bells?
20. [Summer Memory] If you ever experienced genuine warmth—natural summer heat, hot spring water, or magic—how did it feel compared to furnace heat?
III. Daily Life & Senses of Varenhold (21–30)
21. [Morning Sound] What distinctive sound tells you a new day has begun: cathedral tolling bells, iron coal carts on cobblestones, or street hawkers?
22. [City Smell] Varenhold smells of coal soot, river mud, burning amber, and wet wool. Which of these scents do you notice most when stepping outside?
23. [Flickering Omen] When an amber streetlamp suddenly flickers and gutters in an alleyway, what is your instinctive reaction: draw steel, or make a quiet prayer?
24. [Footwear] What state are your boots in? Are they sturdy waxed leather, patched up with rags, or reinforced with iron studs for grip on icy stones?
25. [Timekeeping] How do you keep track of hours and appointments: church bells, a pocket watch, a candle with hourly pins, or pure internal rhythm?
26. [River Fog] When the heavy, sulfurous fog rolls off the Graymere river, does the blindness make you feel vulnerable, or do you welcome the cover?
27. [Street Habits] Do you walk with your eyes locked forward and hood low to avoid attention, or do you scan rooftops and alley mouths as you walk?
28. [Tavern Haven] When you have a spare copper mark and an hour of peace, what kind of tavern do you seek: a boisterous taproom or a quiet corner booth?
29. [Personal Token] What small, seemingly worthless object do you carry in your pocket that you habitually touch when anxious or deep in thought?
30. [The Silence] Late at night when street noise dies down, the silence under the twilight can be oppressive. How do you quiet your mind before sleeping?
IV. Food, Scarcity & Amber Economy (31–40)
31. [The Ration] Fresh fruit and vegetables are legendary luxuries in Varenhold. What common, preserved ration are you utterly sick of eating?
32. [Amber Writs] The Compact taxes and strictly controls amber heating fuel. How do you feel about the merchants and wardens who hoard the warmth?
33. [A Rare Feast] What was the most extravagant or memorable meal you ever tasted in this city, and who were you sharing it with?
34. [Barter] Coin is often useless when supplies run dry. What valuable skill, favor, or item do you trade when people refuse copper marks?
35. [Hunger] Have you ever experienced true, gnawing hunger during a fuel or flour shortage? How did that hunger change the way you look at food?
36. [A Luxury Relic] You come across an artifact from the sunlit era—a parasol, a straw sunhat, or a sundial. Do you find it tragic, beautiful, or laughable?
37. [Hoarding] Do you keep a secret emergency stash of food, coin, or fuel tucked away somewhere, or do you live strictly day-to-day?
38. [Generosity] When you see a freezing beggar or an orphan huddled over a street vent, do you give what you have, or harden your heart to survive?
39. [The Smugglers] Contraband fuel and unregistered amber glass move along the canals every night. Do you view smugglers as criminals, or life-savers?
40. [Windfall] If you suddenly came into fifty gold sovereigns tomorrow, what is the very first thing you would buy for yourself or your companions?
V. The Grey Sickness & Mortality (41–50)
41. [The Ash Cough] The Grey Sickness begins with a dry, chalky cough. Whenever you clear your throat on a cold morning, does a flash of fear cross your mind?
42. [First Witness] Who was the first person you ever saw afflicted by the petrifying touch of the Grey Sickness, and how did watching them affect you?
43. [Folk Remedies] What strange home remedy—vinegar rags, pine pitch tea, crushed birch bark—did someone once claim could ward off the ash?
44. [The Healing House] How do you view the Lowmark Healing House: a heroic sanctuary of compassionate healers, or a grim waystation where people go to die?
45. [Quarantine] If a close ally began showing subtle tremors in their fingers, would you urge them to seek quarantine healers, or help them hide it?
46. [Burial Rites] In Varenhold, the dead are often interred in deep stone crypts to escape the frost. What are your personal thoughts on what lies after death?
47. [Physical Scars] What physical scar or mark on your body carries the most meaningful story, and do you show it openly or keep it covered?
48. [Street Superstition] What morbid rumor or superstition about the Grey Sickness have you overheard in taprooms that you half-suspect might be true?
49. [The Inevitable] In a world where winter never relents and sickness lingers in the fog, what keeps you fighting for tomorrow instead of giving up?
50. [A Healer's Debt] Has a doctor, apothecary, or herbalist ever saved your life or sewn you back together? Did you pay them in full, or do you still owe them?
VI. City Quarters & Local Roots (51–60)
51. [Home Ground] Which quarter of Varenhold feels most familiar to your boots: the crowded Lowmark, the fortified Ashring, or the canal-side docks?
52. [Dreaded District] Which neighborhood or district in the city gives you an uneasy chill whenever you are forced to walk its streets alone?
53. [The High Spire] When you look up at the Spire of Astronomers piercing the overcast, do you see a beacon of ancient wisdom, or a symbol of elite arrogance?
54. [The Canal Bridges] The stone bridges crossing the black canal waters are prime ambush spots. How do you cross a bridge when night falls?
55. [Old Ruins] Varenhold is built atop centuries of older stone. Have you ever explored an old cellar, flooded crypt, or abandoned sewer beneath the streets?
56. [The Outer Walls] Have you ever stood on the city ramparts and looked out into the vast, dark wilderness beyond? Did you feel protected, or trapped?
57. [Favorite View] What is the single most striking or oddly beautiful architectural sight in the city that always catches your eye?
58. [The Watch] When you hear the synchronized boots of an armed city patrol approaching around a corner, is your instinct to stand tall, or step into a doorway?
59. [Safe Haven] Where in Varenhold is the one spot where you can let your guard down completely and feel genuinely safe for a few hours?
60. [The Outside World] Were you born within Varenhold's walls, or did you travel here from the distant parishes or beyond? Do you consider this city your home?
VII. Faith & The Silent Gods (61–70)
61. [Private Prayer] When you find yourself in mortal terror or desperate need, which god or power do you instinctively address in your thoughts?
62. [Auris the Sun] Cathedrals still preach devotion to Auris, the God of the Sun. Do you regard Auris with genuine devotion, anger, or hollow indifference?
63. [Solenne of the Hearth] Solenne represents the hearthfire, domestic warmth, and hospitality. Do you honor the hearth goddess, and how do you show it?
64. [Forgotten Deities] Do you place any faith in older, pre-city spirits—gods of the cold river, the soil, the winter moon, or ancient stone?
65. [Holy Tokens] Do you wear or carry a religious symbol, consecrated prayer strip, or warding talisman? What does it mean to you personally?
66. [The Silence of Gods] For fifty years, prayers for true sunrise have gone unanswered. In your opinion, did the gods turn their backs, or are they weeping with us?
67. [Sacred Ritual] What small personal ritual—lighting a taper, spilling a drop of ale, touching cold iron—do you perform before facing dangerous odds?
68. [Cathedral Echoes] Have you ever stood beneath the towering vaulted ceilings of the Grand Cathedral? Did the grandeur inspire awe, or feel like an empty cage?
69. [Blasphemy] What doubt or irreverent thought about the divine have you harbored in your chest that you would never dare utter aloud in a temple?
70. [A Miracle's Cost] If a priest told you the gods would bring back the sun, but only in exchange for a tremendous civic sacrifice, would you believe them?
VIII. Origins, Mentors & Memories (71–80)
71. [The Mentor] Who taught you your most important skill—how to fight, cast, negotiate, or survive? What was the hardest lesson they drilled into you?
72. [Family Legacy] What trade, reputation, or burden did your family or caregivers leave behind for you in Varenhold?
73. [Childhood Games] What games or mischief did you get up to as a youth in the shadow of the lamps, and who was your partner-in-crime?
74. [An Old Loss] Who from your earlier years do you miss the most, and what is one memory of them that still brings a faint smile to your face?
75. [Elders' Tales] What story told by an elder by the fireside stuck with you so deeply that you still find yourself thinking about it on dark nights?
76. [A Prized Heirlooms] What physical object that you still own has the deepest sentimental value to you, regardless of how much gold it is worth?
77. [Youthful Ambition] What did you dream of becoming before the harsh realities of survival in Varenhold took hold of your life?
78. [A Parting Word] What was the last thing someone important told you before you set out on your current path in life?
79. [First Weapon or Tool] Do you remember the very first weapon, arcane focus, or tool of your trade that felt truly yours? Where did you get it?
80. [A Moment of Laughter] In a city filled with grim faces and cold fog, when was the last time you laughed until your sides hurt, and what caused it?
IX. Fellowship & Companion Bonds (81–90)
81. [The Watcher] Which of your party companions do you feel most comfortable sleeping near when you are on watch during the dead hours?
82. [A Secret Respected] You have noticed that one of your companions carries a private sorrow or secret. Do you respect their silence, or try to draw them out?
83. [Different Minds] Which companion has an outlook on life most opposite to your own, and what about their worldview do you secretly respect?
84. [The Anchor] When chaos erupts in battle or crisis, whose voice among your allies helps you steady your nerves and focus your resolve?
85. [A Shared Meal] Which of your companions would you most trust to cook a meal from scarce rations, and which one would you never let near the pot?
86. [In the Trenches] Think of a recent moment where a companion saved you from injury or humiliation. How did you thank them, if at all?
87. [Worry] Which of your companions do you worry about the most when things turn grim, fearing they might throw their life away for others?
88. [Moral Compass] When a difficult, morally grey decision faces the group, whose judgment among your peers do you weigh most heavily?
89. [Unspoken Bond] What is something you would willingly sacrifice for your companions that you would never admit aloud to them?
90. [Lifting Spirits] When the party is shivering, exhausted, and losing hope, what is your way of trying to restore morale or ease the tension?
X. The Rumor of Dawn & The Cost (91–100)
91. [Belief in Dawn] In your quietest moments, do you genuinely believe a true, radiant sunrise will ever return to this world, or is twilight permanent?
92. [The Dawnborn] People born on the night the sun failed carry lingering dawn in their veins. How do you view them: blessed by light, or cursed by fate?
93. [The Morning After] If the true sun blazed across the mountains tomorrow, what is the very first thing you would do with your newfound day?
94. [A World in Sunlight] Without the daily struggle against cold and darkness, what kind of life would you seek to build for yourself in peace?
95. [Fear of Light] Does the prospect of true daylight frighten you in any way? What secrets or flaws might full sunlight reveal that darkness hides?
96. [The Fall of Amber] If daylight returns and amber fuel becomes worthless, thousands of workers and guilds will be upended. Does change excite or worry you?
97. [The Measure of Sacrifice] If returning the light to Varenhold demanded a heavy, permanent sacrifice from you personally, where would you draw the line?
98. [Free Will] If a person held the power to end the twilight but refused because it would cost them their life, would you force them, or defend their choice?
99. [What Remains] If the dawn is restored, what beauty or quiet kinship forged in this city of shadows will you miss when the twilight is gone?
100. [The Price of Dawn] They say that every dawn has an invoice written in blood, consent, and memory. In your own words, what is dawn worth to you?
