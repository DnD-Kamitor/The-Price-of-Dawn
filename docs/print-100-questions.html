The Price of Dawn — d100 Twilight Character Questions
Varenhold Grounding Table · Roll 1d100 for memories, sensory stakes & moral weight
I. The Lost Sun & Amber Atmosphere (01–10)
01.[Sun]You’ve never felt direct sunlight. What was the wildest thing an elder told you the sun could do?
02.[Blue]When someone says "blue," what shade actually appears in your mind's eye?
03.[Warmth]What is the warmest natural thing you’ve touched that didn't come from an amber furnace?
04.[Wonder]Have you stared directly into an amber lamp until your vision flashed? What did you hope to see?
05.[Dreams]The sky is slate-grey. What is the most vivid dream of a clear sky you’ve ever had?
06.[Blight]When did you first realize plants outside the city grow pale and stunted without real daybreak?
07.[Folklore]An old song describes "shadows that vanish at noon." How does a disappearing shadow make you feel?
08.[Art]In pre-Twilight tapestries, what color looked most unnatural or frightening to you?
09.[Morning]What scent means "morning" to you, since daybreak is only marked by bells and coal smoke?
10.[Price]If promised five minutes of genuine, blazing midday sun, what would you trade for it?
II. Daily Survival & The Cold Reality (11–20)
11.[Omen]What small superstition do you observe whenever an amber streetlamp flickers?
12.[Food]Your go-to comfort food: salted cap-mushrooms, tallow bread, river eel, or chicory brew?
13.[Time]How do you tell day from night when your clock is exhausted and the sky never changes?
14.[Fog]Which garment of yours is permanently damp at the cuffs from river fog?
15.[Waking]What sound wakes you: Lowmark coal carts, cathedral bells, or Reckoning patrol boots?
16.[Relic]You fiddle with a pocket token when anxious. What is it, and who gave it to you?
17.[Tavern]Favorite hole-in-the-wall tavern where the hearthfire is actually kept warm?
18.[Hiding]Where in Varenhold do you go to be totally alone, where not even orphans find you?
19.[Chill]How many layers of wool or leather do you wear daily to keep frost from your joints?
20.[Warmth]Most expensive thing you bought this year purely to keep from freezing?
III. Sickness, Frost & The Healers (21–30)
21.[Grief]Who was the first person you watched develop the chalky cough of Stage-1 Grey Sickness?
22.[Remedy]What foul tincture or herbal brew does your family swear keeps the ash out of lungs?
23.[Terror]When you cough at dawn, do you secretly check your handkerchief for ash before pocketing it?
24.[Clinic]Ever entered the Lowmark Healing House? What smell hit you first inside?
25.[Winter]Coldest night of your life: who or what kept you from freezing in your sleep?
26.[Quarantine]If a close friend hid a Stage-2 tremor, would you report them or shelter them?
27.[Rhymes]Cruelest joke or rhyme street children sing about people turning ash-grey?
28.[Dawnborn]Ever stood near a Dawnborn and felt the air heat up? What went through your mind?
29.[Scars]Which old scar or bone ache flares worst whenever northern frost rolls in?
30.[Draught]If a potion guaranteed frost immunity for 3 months' rations, would you buy it?
IV. Amber Economy & Fuel (31–40)
31.[Chits]How many amber ration chits do you currently have sewn into your coat lining?
32.[Lies]Cleverest lie you told a quartermaster to get an extra measure of fuel or grain?
33.[Theft]Ever stolen an amber bead from a lamp or shrine? Did you keep it or sell it?
34.[Waste]What pre-Twilight luxury seems utterly ridiculous and wasteful to you now?
35.[Smuggle]The Compact taxes amber glass. Ever helped someone smuggle glass across canals?
36.[Debt]What item did you inherit from someone who couldn't pay their Compact heating debt?
37.[Fortune]With 100 gold: buy land outside walls, hoard amber crystals, or hire a carriage out?
38.[Allies]Which merchant knows your name and cuts you a break when times are tight?
39.[Dignity]Lowest price you’ve seen someone sell their pride for during a bread famine?
40.[Lantern]Have you ever owned your own amber lamp, or always had to borrow light?
V. City Quarters, Streets & Shadows (41–50)
41.[Dread]Which district makes your neck hairs rise when walking it after fourth bell?
42.[Alley]Favorite Ashring shortcut through back alleys that the city watch doesn't know?
43.[Spire]Looked up at the Spire catwalks: what do you think astronomers are hiding up there?
44.[Tunnels]What rumor about the subterranean tunnels beneath canal locks do you half-believe?
45.[Brawls]In which tavern was your first bar brawl, and what petty argument sparked it?
46.[Home]Which neighborhood felt like home when you were young, and how has it decayed?
47.[Granite]Oldest piece of pre-Twilight stone architecture you pass daily without noticing?
48.[Gates]If gates barred for permanent quarantine tomorrow: caught inside or outside?
49.[Beauty]Most beautiful sight you’ve seen in Varenhold despite the perpetual gloom?
50.[Sages]Which beggar or street character do you toss coin to, and what story do they tell?
VI. Factions & Divided Loyalties (51–60)
51.[Patrol]Your closest call with a Reckoning patrol demanding papers or inspection?
52.[Harran]Is Harran Lecht a tyrant trying to enslave the city, or a desperate man holding back the dark?
53.[Vigils]Ever attended Brother Edoran’s midnight lantern vigils? Did you feel holy or uneasy?
54.[Dawnless]The Dawnless are silent. What rumor about their severed names scares you most?
55.[Blades]If Solennite Blades offered coin to spy on companions, would you refuse or hesitate?
56.[Divided]What friend or kin joined the Reckoning, and can you still look them in the eye?
57.[Rot]Most corrupt act by a Councillor or Guildmaster you witnessed in broad daylight?
58.[Shadar-kai]Ever caught the gaze of Concordance priests watching from dark alleys?
59.[Zealots]Which faction is most dangerous because they genuinely believe they are the heroes?
60.[Alliance]Who do you trust with your life: Ostenveld, Harran Lecht, or Brother Edoran?
VII. Faith, Gods & Prayers (61–70)
61.[Prayers]Praying in the dark: Auris the Wounded Sun, Solenne of the Hearth, or forgotten gods?
62.[Silence]Did the gods abandon Varenhold for Corven’s sin, or were they powerless to stop it?
63.[Charms]What holy symbol or consecrated token do you carry, and where did you get it?
64.[Blasphemy]Most blasphemous thought about the sun god while shivering by an amber lamp?
65.[Cathedral]Ever lit a taper in Auris Cathedral watching wax drip on cold stone? What did you pray for?
66.[Pulpit]When priests promise "radiant dawn for the righteous," do you feel comforted or bitter?
67.[Afterlife]Do souls who die under twilight reach the afterlife, or wander the cold mist?
68.[Festival]What seasonal festival do you still keep, even though astronomical seasons are gone?
69.[Shrines]Strangest wayside shrine you’ve encountered out in Ashfen marsh or Dusk Parishes?
70.[Demand]Facing Auris directly: what angry or desperate question would you demand answered?
VIII. Family, Memory & Childhood (71–80)
71.[Games]Childhood street game that required hiding in deep shadow between lamp pools?
72.[Orchards]Which elder wouldn't stop raving about how green apples were before Year of Ash?
73.[Shields]What did parents try to shield you from that you discovered anyway before twelve?
74.[Lost Kin]Childhood friend who vanished to sickness, moved away, or joined a hated faction?
75.[Lies]First lie your parents told you about twilight so you wouldn't fear the dark?
76.[Keepsake]What possession of your mother or father do you still carry everywhere?
77.[Hearth]Grew up with ample coal, or in a drafty tenement sleeping with all clothes on?
78.[Silence]Last thing you said to someone in your family before bitter silence fell?
79.[Lessons]What lesson drilled by a mentor has kept you alive when others died?
80.[Joy]When did you last laugh until ribs hurt, and who was in the room with you?
IX. Moral Compromise & Guilt (81–90)
81.[Crime]Worst thing you did to ensure you or a loved one had fuel or food for winter?
82.[Scale]Sacrifice one innocent to restore sun to 300,000: where does your gut land?
83.[Past]A secret about your history you pray your party companions never uncover?
84.[Oaths]Have you broken a solemn oath because keeping it would have ruined you?
85.[Failure]Who is the person you secretly believe you failed when they needed you most?
86.[Trust]Which party member do you trust unconditionally to pull you from fire?
87.[Burdens]Which companion do you fear is carrying a secret burden that will snap them?
88.[The Line]What moral line will you never cross, no matter how desperate the stakes?
89.[Conscience]Killing an enemy in the dark: what do you tell yourself to wash your conscience?
90.[Unsaid]If you died tonight in the Ashring, what would you regret leaving unsaid?
X. Dawnborn, Sacrifice & Climax (91–100)
91.[Dawnborn]Ten people carrying the trapped dawn in their blood: do you envy or pity them?
92.[Tomas]Tomas Areth obsessively checking star notes for 40 years: what would you say to him?
93.[Aldric]Aldric Stone’s glowing amber eyes: does being near genuine heat unnerve you?
94.[Free Will]A Dawnborn says: "I don't want to die for your city." Could you force them on the stone?
95.[Day After]The day after sun returns: will people dance in streets, or turn on each other?
96.[Economy]Amber guilds lose everything overnight when sun returns. Whose ruin can you watch?
97.[Peace]What will you do with your life if you no longer fight to survive every day?
98.[Fear of Dawn]Most terrifying thing about real sun: afraid you won't recognize yourself in light?
99.[Ixa]Ixa’s hands are always warm. What part of her survival echoes your own?
100.[The Price]If dawn has a price, and someone in this room must pay it... why not you?
