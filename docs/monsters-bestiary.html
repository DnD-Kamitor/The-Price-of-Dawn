38.2 Section 1: The Climax & Session 5 Encounters

38.2.1 Commander Harran Lecht

Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 17 (scarred half-plate) - Hit Points: 102 (12d8 + 48) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 18 (+4) | DEX: 12 (+1) | CON: 18 (+4) | INT: 12 (+1) | WIS: 10 (+0) | CHA: 16 (+3) - Saving Throws: Str +7, Con +7, Wis +3 - Skills: Athletics +7, Intimidation +6, Perception +3 - Condition Immunities: Charmed, frightened - Senses: Passive Perception 13 - Languages: Common - Challenge: 6 (2,300 XP)

Traits: - Desperate Conviction: While below 50 HP (bloodied), Harran gains a +2 bonus to all attack rolls and deals an additional 4 (1d8) damage with his broadsword. His armor is battered and his breath is ragged, but he refuses to stop. - Iron Command: Allied soldiers within 30 ft of Harran add +1d4 to their attack rolls and cannot be charmed or frightened while Harran is conscious. - The Unforgiving Choice: Harran cannot be persuaded to abandon his attack with words alone; only reducing him to 0 HP or completing the stone circle before his eyes will break his conviction.

Actions: - Multiattack: Harran makes three attacks: two with his Heavy Broadsword and one with his Shield Slam. - Heavy Broadsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit (+9 if bloodied), reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage, plus 4 (1d8) slashing damage if bloodied. - Shield Slam: Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. Target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and pushed 5 ft back.

Bonus Actions: - Rally the Lost (Recharge 5–6): As a bonus action, Harran roars a battle cry: “Hold the line! For Varenhold!” Up to four allied soldiers within 30 ft immediately gain 15 temporary hit points and can move up to their speed toward the dais without provoking opportunity attacks.

Reactions: - Unyielding Parry: Harran adds +3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, he must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

GM Tactics & Morale: > Charges directly for the center dais. Knocks party defenders away with Shield Slam so his soldiers can disrupt the Dawnborn. At bloodied (under 50 HP), he ignores opportunity attacks to reach whoever holds Corven’s box. When reduced to 0 HP, he collapses to his knees; if the circle is glowing gold, his will breaks in awe.

38.2.2 Lieutenant Senna Kard

Medium humanoid (human), neutral good (conflicted) - Armor Class: 16 (studded leather + buckler) - Hit Points: 78 (12d8 + 24) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 14 (+2) | DEX: 16 (+3) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 12 (+1) | WIS: 14 (+2) | CHA: 15 (+2) - Saving Throws: Dex +5, Wis +4 - Skills: Insight +5, Perception +4, Persuasion +4 - Senses: Passive Perception 14 - Languages: Common - Challenge: 5 (1,800 XP)

Traits: - Hesitant Blade: Senna does not want to slaughter civilians or Dawnborn. She fights defensively. If a PC uses their action to speak to her about the cost of the fight or her fear of meaningless bloodshed, she has disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of her next turn. - Protective Discipline: Allied soldiers within 15 ft of Senna gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws.

Actions: - Multiattack: Senna makes three Officer’s Sabre attacks. - Officer’s Sabre: Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage. If targeting a Dawnborn, Senna always chooses non-lethal damage. - Order: Stand Down (1/Battle): Triggered by a successful DC 15 Persuasion check addressing her conscience. Senna lowers her blade and shouts: “Hold your steel! We are not butchers!” All remaining Veterans take the Dodge action and cease attacking.

Bonus Actions: - Rally the Guard (Recharge 5–6): Senna shouts tactical orders. Up to three allied soldiers within 30 ft gain 10 temporary hit points and can move up to 15 ft without provoking opportunity attacks.

Reactions: - Parry: Senna adds +3 to her AC against one melee attack that would hit her.

GM Tactics & Morale: > Stays 20 ft behind the vanguard, ordering soldiers to disarm and drag Dawnborn rather than slaughter. Fights defensively. If targeted by emotional appeals or confronted with civilian casualties, she can be persuaded to order a ceasefire (DC 15 Persuasion).

38.2.3 Reckoning Veteran

Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 17 (splint mail) - Hit Points: 58 (9d8 + 18) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 16 (+3) | DEX: 13 (+1) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 10 (+0) | WIS: 11 (+0) | CHA: 10 (+0) - Skills: Athletics +5, Perception +2 - Senses: Passive Perception 12 - Languages: Common - Challenge: 3 (700 XP)

Traits: - Shield Wall: The Veteran gains a +1 bonus to AC (included) for each allied soldier wielding a shield within 5 ft (maximum +2). - Disciplined Advance: The Veteran has advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone or moved against their will while adjacent to an ally.

Actions: - Multiattack: The Veteran makes two Longsword attacks or two Heavy Crossbow attacks. - Longsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) if two-handed. - Heavy Crossbow: Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.

Bonus Actions: - Disrupt the Circle: When adjacent to a Dawnborn or ritual participant, the Veteran attempts an Athletics check (contested by target’s Str/Dex save, DC 13) to drag or shove that person up to 10 ft away from their stone.

Reactions: - Interpose Shield: When an adjacent ally is targeted by an attack, the Veteran imposes disadvantage on the attack roll.

38.2.4 Solennite Blade Infiltrator

Medium humanoid (half-elf), neutral evil - Armor Class: 15 (studded leather) - Hit Points: 44 (8d8 + 8) - Speed: 35 ft. - STR: 11 (+0) | DEX: 16 (+3) | CON: 12 (+1) | INT: 12 (+1) | WIS: 10 (+0) | CHA: 12 (+1) - Saving Throws: Dex +5 - Skills: Acrobatics +5, Stealth +7, Sleight of Hand +5 - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12 - Languages: Common, Elvish - Challenge: 2 (450 XP)

Traits: - Saboteur’s Precision: Weapon attacks deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage against inanimate ritual objects, stone conduits, and puzzle housings. - Sneak Attack (1/Turn): Deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if it has advantage on the attack roll or an ally is within 5 ft.

Actions: - Shortsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage + 3 (1d6) poison damage. - Hand Crossbow: Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

Bonus Actions: - Cunning Action: The Blade can take Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.

Reactions: - Shadow Slip: When targeted by a melee attack, the Blade can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on the roll by darting into a nearby shadow.

38.2.5 Grasping Tendril

Medium aberration (formerly humanoid), unaligned - Armor Class: 14 (natural armor) - Hit Points: 91 (14d8 + 28) - Speed: 35 ft., climb 20 ft. - STR: 18 (+4) | DEX: 16 (+3) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 6 (-2) | WIS: 10 (+0) | CHA: 4 (-3) - Saving Throws: Str +7, Con +5 - Skills: Athletics +7, Stealth +6 - Damage Resistances: Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks - Condition Immunities: Charmed, frightened - Senses: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10 - Languages: Understands Common but cannot speak - Challenge: 6 (2,300 XP)

Traits: - Target Lock (Ixa): The Tendril is hardwired to retrieve Ixa. It cannot be taunted or charmed away from her. If Ixa breaks line of sight, it spends its full movement each turn to reacquire her. - Flexible Form: Can squeeze through spaces as tight as 1 ft wide without penalty. - Pack Retrieval: When two or more Tendrils are within 5 ft of the same grappled target, both have advantage on Athletics checks to maintain or move the grapple. - Not Dead: At 0 HP, the Tendril does not die—it collapses, paralyzed but conscious. A DC 14 Medicine check reveals it was once a human citizen, altered to the bone by surgical horror.

Actions: - Multiattack: Two Grasping Slam attacks, or one Grasping Slam and one Constrict. - Grasping Slam: Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If target is Large or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 16). Can grapple up to two creatures at once. - Constrict: Target must already be grappled. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage and must succeed on DC 15 Str save or be restrained until grapple ends.

38.3 Section 2: The Concordance & Session 4 Foes

38.3.1 Null-Warden

Medium humanoid (shadar-kai), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 19 (obsidian-etched plate armor) - Hit Points: 161 (19d8 + 76) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 20 (+5) | DEX: 14 (+2) | CON: 18 (+4) | INT: 16 (+3) | WIS: 16 (+3) | CHA: 10 (+0) - Saving Throws: Str +9, Con +8, Wis +7 - Skills: Arcana +7, Athletics +9, Perception +7 - Damage Resistances: Necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks - Condition Immunities: Charmed, frightened - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17 - Languages: Common, Elvish, Undercommon - Challenge: 11 (7,200 XP)

Traits: - Null Aura: Creatures within 10 ft of the Warden cannot cast spells of 1st level or higher without succeeding on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On failure, the spell slot is expended and the spell fails. - Magical Resistance: Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects. - Inversion Anchor: When standing on or within 10 ft of a ritual plinth, the Warden is immune to forced movement and cannot be knocked prone.

Actions: - Multiattack: Three attacks with its Null Rod. - Null Rod: Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage + 9 (2d8) necrotic damage. If target is concentrating on a spell, the DC to maintain concentration is at least 15. - Sever Resonance (Recharge 5–6): The Warden strikes the stone floor. A shockwave of null-magic ripples out in a 20-ft radius. All creatures must make a DC 16 Constitution save, taking 28 (8d6) force damage on failure (half on success), and any active magical buff of 3rd level or lower is dispelled.

Bonus Actions: - Shadow Step (Recharge 4–6): Teleports up to 30 ft between dim or dark spaces with advantage on its next melee attack.

Reactions: - Spellcrusher: When a creature within 5 ft casts a spell, the Warden makes a Null Rod attack against that creature. If it hits, the caster must make a DC 16 Con save or the spell is ruined.

38.3.2 Priest of the Inward Crescent

Medium humanoid (shadar-kai), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 14 (chain shirt) - Hit Points: 97 (15d8 + 30) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 10 (+0) | DEX: 14 (+2) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 16 (+3) | WIS: 18 (+4) | CHA: 13 (+1) - Saving Throws: Int +6, Wis +7 - Skills: Arcana +6, Insight +7, Perception +7, Religion +6 - Damage Resistances: Necrotic - Condition Immunities: Charmed - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17 - Languages: Common, Elvish, Undercommon - Challenge: 7 (2,900 XP)

Traits: - Suppression Ritual: While two Priests are within 10 ft of each other, they pour obsidian powder to sever the stone’s resonance. Breaking concentration requires 20+ damage in a single hit or physically disrupting the powder line. - Doctrinal Certainty: Advantage on saves vs spells that compel truth or alter belief.

Actions: - Multiattack: Two attacks: Toll the Dead + Spiritual Weapon, or two Inflict Wounds. - Inflict Wounds: Melee Spell Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 22 (4d10) necrotic damage. - Toll the Dead (Cantrip): DC 15 Wis save or 9 (2d8) necrotic, or 13 (2d12) if missing any HP. - Spirit Guardians (3rd Slot, Conc.): 15-ft aura of necrotic spirits: DC 15 Wis save or take 18 (4d8) necrotic upon entering/starting turn. - Banishment (4th Slot, Conc.): DC 15 Cha save or banished to harmless demiplane for 1 minute.

Bonus Actions: - Spiritual Weapon: Floating obsidian blade: +7 to hit, 1d8 + 4 force damage. - Shadow Step (Recharge 5–6): Teleports up to 30 ft between dim/dark spaces.

Reactions: - Counterspell (3rd Slot): Interrupts a spell of 3rd level or lower within 60 ft.

38.3.3 Unlit Shadow-Keeper

Medium humanoid (shadar-kai), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 17 (chain mail + shield) - Hit Points: 112 (15d8 + 45) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 16 (+3) | DEX: 16 (+3) | CON: 16 (+3) | INT: 14 (+2) | WIS: 16 (+3) | CHA: 12 (+1) - Saving Throws: Str +6, Con +6, Wis +6 - Skills: Athletics +6, Insight +6, Perception +6, Stealth +6 - Damage Resistances: Necrotic - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16 - Languages: Common, Elvish, Undercommon - Challenge: 8 (3,900 XP)

Traits: - Cell Command: When the Shadow-Keeper uses Shadow Step, all allied Operatives within 30 ft may use their reaction to make one weapon attack. - Twilight Awareness: Cannot be surprised in dim light or darkness. Perceives invisible creatures within 15 ft as blurred silhouettes (DC 14 to pinpoint). - Doctrine Hold: Immune to spells that read thoughts or compel truth.

Actions: - Multiattack: Three Shortsword attacks or two Shortsword + one Shadow Drain. - Shortsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing + 7 (2d6) necrotic. - Shadow Drain: Ranged Spell Attack: +6 to hit, range 60 ft. Hit: 14 (4d6) necrotic damage and target’s speed is halved until end of next turn. - Enveloping Dark (Recharge 5–6): 15-ft radius: dim light becomes darkness for 1 minute (concentration). Non-shadar-kai must make DC 14 Con save or be blinded until leaving the area.

38.3.4 Unlit Doctrinal Voice

Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 13 (mage armor) - Hit Points: 84 (13d8 + 26) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 10 (+0) | DEX: 13 (+1) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 18 (+4) | WIS: 16 (+3) | CHA: 14 (+2) - Saving Throws: Int +7, Wis +6 - Skills: Arcana +7, History +7, Insight +6, Persuasion +5 - Condition Immunities: Charmed - Senses: Passive Perception 13 - Languages: Common, Elvish, Dwarvish, Infernal - Challenge: 7 (2,900 XP)

Traits: - Doctrinal Certainty (3/Day): Legendary Resistance against spells attempting to alter beliefs, charm, or compel action. - Back Line: Never voluntarily moves within 30 ft of a player character.

Actions: - Toll the Dead: Range 60 ft: DC 15 Wis save or 9 (2d8) necrotic, or 13 (2d12) if damaged. - Hold Person (2nd Slot): DC 15 Wis save or paralyzed for 1 min. - Hypnotic Pattern (3rd Slot): 30-ft cube within 120 ft: DC 15 Wis save or incapacitated (speed 0) until damaged. - Fear (3rd Slot): 30-ft cone: DC 15 Wis save or frightened and dashes away. - Banishment (4th Slot): DC 15 Cha save or banished 1 min. - Greater Invisibility (4th Slot): Becomes invisible for 1 min (concentration).

Reactions: - Counterspell (3rd Slot): Negates a spell within 60 ft. Prioritizes countering healing or dispel magic. - Shield (1st Slot): +5 to AC until start of next turn, blocks Magic Missile.

38.3.5 Unlit Operative

Medium humanoid (shadar-kai), lawful neutral - Armor Class: 15 (studded leather) - Hit Points: 65 (10d8 + 20) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 12 (+1) | DEX: 18 (+4) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 13 (+1) | WIS: 14 (+2) | CHA: 10 (+0) - Saving Throws: Dex +7, Wis +5 - Skills: Athletics +4, Deception +3, Perception +5, Stealth +9 - Damage Resistances: Necrotic - Condition Immunities: Charmed, frightened - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15 - Languages: Common, Elvish - Challenge: 5 (1,800 XP)

Traits: - Pack Tactics: Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft of the creature. - Cell Discipline: Cannot reveal names or locations above their cell—they have been mind-blanked.

Actions: - Multiattack: Two Shortsword attacks or two Hand Crossbow attacks. - Shortsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage. - Hand Crossbow: Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 30/120 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.

Bonus Actions: - Shadow Step (Recharge 4–6): Teleports up to 30 ft between dim/dark spaces.

Reactions: - Fade: When reduced to 0 HP, makes one final reflexive Shadow Step to nearby cover before falling unconscious, leaving no trail.

38.4 Section 3: Shadows of Varenhold & Earlier Encounters

38.4.1 Dusk Priest

Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil - Armor Class: 14 (studded leather) - Hit Points: 110 (17d8 + 34) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 12 (+1) | DEX: 15 (+2) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 14 (+2) | WIS: 18 (+4) | CHA: 14 (+2) - Saving Throws: Wis +7, Cha +5 - Skills: Perception +7, Religion +5, Stealth +5 - Damage Resistances: Necrotic - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17 - Languages: Common, Elvish - Challenge: 7 (2,900 XP)

Traits: - Twilight Authority: Undead allies within 30 ft have advantage on saving throws against being turned.

Actions: - Multiattack: Cast Toll the Dead + one Dagger attack, or cast two spells. - Sacrificial Dagger: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing + 10 (3d6) necrotic. - Toll the Dead: Range 60 ft: DC 15 Wis save or 9 (2d8) necrotic, or 13 (2d12) if damaged. - Spirit Guardians: DC 15 Wis save or 18 (4d8) necrotic aura.

Reactions: - Shield of the Dark: When hit by an attack, gains +3 AC until end of turn.

38.4.2 The Returned (Concordance Ghast)

Medium undead, chaotic evil - Armor Class: 13 (natural armor) - Hit Points: 65 (10d8 + 20) - Speed: 30 ft. - STR: 17 (+3) | DEX: 13 (+1) | CON: 15 (+2) | INT: 10 (+0) | WIS: 10 (+0) | CHA: 8 (-1) - Saving Throws: Con +4 - Skills: Perception +2 - Damage Resistances: Necrotic - Damage Immunities: Poison - Condition Immunities: Charmed, exhaustion, poisoned, paralyzed - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12 - Languages: Understands Common but cannot speak - Challenge: 3 (700 XP)

Traits: - Stench: Creatures starting turn within 5 ft must make DC 13 Con save or be poisoned until start of next turn. Success grants 24h immunity. - Pack Tactics: Advantage on attack rolls when an ally is within 5 ft of target. - Turning Defiance: Advantage on saving throws against effects that turn undead.

Actions: - Multiattack: One Bite and one Claws attack. - Bite: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage. - Claws: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage. Target must succeed on DC 13 Con save or be paralyzed until end of next turn.

38.4.3 Cinder-of-Ashfall

Medium humanoid (tabaxi), neutral - Armor Class: 14 (leather armor) - Hit Points: 52 (8d8 + 16) - Speed: 30 ft., climb 20 ft. - STR: 11 (+0) | DEX: 18 (+4) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 13 (+1) | WIS: 12 (+1) | CHA: 10 (+0) - Saving Throws: Dex +6, Int +3 - Skills: Acrobatics +6, Perception +5, Stealth +8 - Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15 - Languages: Common, Thieves’ Cant - Challenge: 3 (700 XP)

Traits: - Feline Agility: Doubles speed until end of turn. Recharges when she moves 0 ft on a turn. - Sneak Attack (1/Turn): Deals extra 10 (3d6) damage to a target she hits with a finesse or ranged weapon with advantage. - Evasion: Dex save success = 0 damage, failure = half damage.

Actions: - Multiattack: Two Shortsword attacks or two Hand Crossbow attacks. - Shortsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage. - Hand Crossbow: Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 30/120 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.

Bonus Actions: - Cunning Action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide as bonus action.
