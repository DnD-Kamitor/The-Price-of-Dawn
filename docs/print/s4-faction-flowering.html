THE FLOWERING
- - - - - - - - - - - - -
Recovered correspondence. Found pressed inside a book left in an unusual location. The flower attached has one petal too many. The center is darker than it should be.
To the one who should not exist, but does -
We have been looking for you for a long time. Not to harm you. We want you to understand that first, before anything else. Not to harm you. Never to harm you.
You are what love makes when love refuses to be told no. You are proof that the lines the world draws are not laws. They are suggestions. They are the world's fear, wearing the mask of the world's rules.
We helped someone once. A woman in this city, many years ago, who loved someone the world said she was not permitted to love - not in the way she did, not with the outcome she wanted. We showed her it was possible. You are the outcome.
We have been trying to make more outcomes like you. We have not always succeeded. The ones who did not succeed are still with us. We do not leave them. We do not abandon what we begin. We tend what grows, in whatever shape it grows.
You belong with us. Not because we need you - though we do, we will be honest, we need you very much - but because we are the only ones who will look at what you are and not look away.
We are in this city. We have been watching you since you arrived. Come to us. Or simply stop walking away. We will find each other. We always do.
With love that does not stop,
[no signature. a pressed flower is attached. the petals are one too many. the center is dark.]
- - - - - - - - - - - - -
On approach: The Flowering does not operate through open channels. It approaches through people already known to the target - a familiar face, a gift left somewhere personal, a letter that could only come from someone watching closely. They do not fight directly. What they send to address problems is not them. If you want contact deliberately, leave a bloom with a dark center somewhere exposed. Be prepared for warmth that feels slightly wrong.
