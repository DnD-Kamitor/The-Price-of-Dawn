Faction 1: Deluded Light Fanatics

Identity: Mostly human zealots (The Reckoning). Their facts are backwards—they genuinely believe forcing the dawn or sacrificing Dawnborn is heroic martyrdom.

Table Dynamic: Driven by tragic conviction, not evil. They fight with military discipline and righteous desperation, but can be morally broken or reasoned with (Senna Kard).

Faction 2: The Undead (Concordance)

Identity: Deathless covenant ruled by Arch-Lich Vhol. They embrace the eternal twilight as their ascension and use null-magic to sever the sun forever.

Table Dynamic: Cold, methodical, lethal. They employ anti-magic Null Auras, paralyzing claws, necrosis, and shadow-stepping inquisitors to silence resistance.

Faction 3: The Flowering (Mutants)

Identity: Underground bio-magical flesh-crafters led by The Gardener. They view physical laws as flaws to be overcome through mutation and horrific grafting.

Table Dynamic: Body-horror aberrations. Grasping rubbery tendrils, spore-spitters, multi-armed brutes, and relentless regenerating flesh seeking to reclaim Ixa.

The Reckoning: Battlefield Morale & De-Escalation

The Core Tragic Lie: The Reckoning believes the Dawnborn are 'hoarding the sun's stolen essence.' They think slaying or forcing them into the stone circle will release the dawn and feed the starving city.

Shield Wall Tactics: Veterans and Militiamen advance in tight interlocking formations. Crossbow Marksmen provide suppressive cover fire to pin party casters.

The Moral De-escalation:
• Lieutenant Senna Kard (DC 15 Persuasion): If confronted with civilian casualties or shown Corven's peaceful defense, she orders her soldiers to stand down.
• Commander Harran Lecht (Unforgiving): Cannot be talked down while conscious. When reduced to 0 HP, seeing the golden dawn aura awaken the circle shatters his fanatical delusion in tears.
