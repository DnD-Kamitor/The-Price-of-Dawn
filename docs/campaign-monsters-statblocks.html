Session 5: The Climax Plaza

Goal: Stop Harran Lecht's vanguard from breaking Corven's ritual while preventing the Tendril from dragging Ixa into the sunken conduits.

Ritual Clock: 4 complete rounds of concentration (DC 10 Con check if damaged). A DC 15 Persuasion check causes Senna Kard to order a ceasefire on Round 4.

Session 4: The Concordance Core

Goal: Infiltrate the subterranean inversion chamber, neutralize the Null-Warden and Inward Crescent ritualists, and retrieve the Prime Core.

Null Field: Spellcasters within 10 ft of the Null-Warden must succeed on DC 15 Con checks to cast spells of 1st level or higher.

Earlier: Shadows of Varenhold

Goal: Survive the Tannery ambush by Dusk Priests and Ghasts, negotiate with Cinder-of-Ashfall, and bypass the ancient Vault-Warden.

Chasm Bridge: The Vault-Warden can be reasoned with (DC 13 Persuasion) if Ixa is present or by speaking the Elder Futhark password.

Combat Flow & Tactical Directives: The Climax Plaza

Wave 1 (The Tendril Ambush): As the ritual begins, Grasping Tendrils erupt from sunken stone vents. Their hardwired target is Ixa (carrying the Prime Core). Tendrils attempt to grapple and drag her 10 ft/round toward the perimeter.

Wave 2 (The Reckoning Vanguard): On Round 3, Commander Harran Lecht and Lieutenant Senna Kard charge the dais with 4 Reckoning Veterans and 2 Solennite Blades. Veterans advance in a shield wall to pin frontliners.

Dynamic Battlefield & Win Conditions:
• Ritual Activation: Corven must maintain concentration for 4 full rounds to stabilize the Dawn Core.
• The Moral Pivot: A DC 15 Persuasion check targeting Senna Kard causes her to lower her blade. If shown civilian casualties or Corven's defensive stance, she orders a ceasefire on Round 4.
• Harran's Last Stand: Harran cannot be persuaded while conscious. When reduced to 0 HP, he collapses to his knees; seeing the golden light awaken the stone circle breaks his fanatic conviction.

The Concordance Chamber: Lair Actions & Hazards

• Null Inversion Pulse: Obsidian conduits hum with negative energy. Each non-shadar-kai creature within 60 ft must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have its magical healing halved and suffer disadvantage on concentration checks until the next initiative count 20.

The Sunken Tannery: Hazards & Tactical Notes

Atmospheric Hazards:
• Acid Vats: Three massive stone vats contain caustic tannery runoff. A creature shoved or falling into a vat takes 14 (4d6) acid damage at the start of its turn. Climbing out requires 10 ft of movement and a DC 12 Athletics check.
• Slick Flagstones: The floor is coated in grease and slime. Any creature moving more than half speed must succeed on a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.

Encounter Tactics:
• The Dusk Priest uses Spirit Guardians to create a lethal 15-ft killzone while The Returned flank around the acid vats, attempting to paralyze frontliners with their claws.
• The Dusk Priest prioritizes casting Toll the Dead on paralyzed targets for guaranteed maximum critical damage.

Tolvin Heuschel's Bridge: Chasm Hazards & RP Hooks

The Chasm Bridge (Bridge of the Pale Watch):
• Precarious Crossing: The narrow archway is 80 ft long, 10 ft wide, without railings. A 60-ft plunge into the jagged aqueduct deals 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage.
• Warden's Challenge: The Vault-Warden blocks the midpoint. It cannot leave the bridge (Bridge Lock). If the party approaches peacefully, it speaks in archaic Elder Futhark: 'Who approaches the resting stone of the Sun?'
• Social Resolution: If Ixa is present or a PC succeeds on a DC 13 Persuasion check citing the restoration of the light, the Warden deactivates and steps aside.

Cinder-of-Ashfall Roleplay & Bargain:
• Cinder is a pragmatic tabaxi rogue hired by Keseph Vyne's network. She has no loyalty to fanaticism and surrenders if reduced to 15 HP.
• In exchange for safe passage out of the city, she provides secret maps of the subterranean duct system leading directly under the Concordance dais.
