Declaration of Filing

The following document was composed by Archmagister Corven on the night of the Ritual, Year 0 P.R., and deposited in the Sealed Archive by his own hand before the morning of Year 1 P.R. The document was transferred to Restricted Series CR-44 in Year 2 P.R. by order of the Provisional Authority. Its contents have not been reviewed by any serving Archivist since that transfer order was issued.

Three witnesses attested the original document at the time of filing. Their signatures appear below in the order they were given. The document was sealed with Corven's personal mark: a circle enclosing a small sun. The seal was intact at the time of the cover sheet addition in Year 23 P.R.

This cover sheet was added in Year 23 P.R. by Archivist Lenn, on discovery that the original filing lacked a public index entry. The contents of CR-44-09 were not examined at that time. The decision to leave the seal intact was recorded and signed at Senior Archivist level. A note in the restricted index reads: "contents unknown — do not open without authorisation."

Astronomical Timing

The Dawnmark rises when the anchor holds. The five primary anchor positions (I, III, V, VII, IX) correspond to the five stars of the Dawnmark constellation: Warden's Star, Pale Fisher, Shepherd's Eye, Vael's Crown, and Trailing Light. These positions are fixed by the original ritual structure. They cannot be changed, moved, or substituted.

The constellation reaches the required overhead position at 02:14 on the date projected as Year 48 P.R. The Pale Fisher rises first. The Trailing Light sets last. Order matters.
