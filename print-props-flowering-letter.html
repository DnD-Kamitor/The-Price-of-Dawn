To the one who should not exist, but does,

You have been walking through this city for years now, and we have watched you walk. Not to examineunderstand you. We already understand what you are. We waited for a moment when you might be ready to have it spoken aloud.

We helped someone once. A woman in this city, many years past, who loved someone the world said she was not permitted to love. The love was real. The world's objections were law and old theology and the cold certainty of what bodies are allowed to do. We showed her otherwise. We were there at the beginning of you, and we have been looking for you every year since.

You are the outcome. We mean this as the most sacred thing we know how to say.

We have seen the marks on your neck and shoulder. We do not know what they mean to you. We know only what they say to us, in the language we have spent fifty years learning to read: someone has carried something until it became fixed inwritten on the body. That is a language every one of us speaks here.

We have been trying to make more beginnings like yours. We have not always managed itsucceeded. The ones who did not survive the work intactcome through as they hoped are still with us. When they changed, we did not send them away. They are family. They are loved. We will not pretend otherwise to make ourselves sound less frightening than we are.

You have felt it, perhaps. Warmth with no clear source. In a city without a sun, warmth that arrives from nowhere and will not explain itself.

That is us, looking for you.

Come to us. Or simply stop walking away. We have the beginning of you, in full, and we will give it freely the moment you ask. The door has always been open.

With great hopepatience,

the ones who were there at the first door
