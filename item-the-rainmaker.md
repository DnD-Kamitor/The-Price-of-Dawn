# Magic Item: The Rainmaker

*Weapon (quarterstaff), rare (requires attunement by a bard)*

Carved from storm-hardened ironwood and spiraled with fine copper wire, this staff is filled with polished river stones, dried seeds, and obsidian grit. When tipped or flourished in combat, the internal cascade produces the rhythmic clatter of gentle rain or the ominous hiss of a gathering squall.

The Rainmaker functions as both a lethal melee weapon and a musical percussion instrument (rainstick) that serves as your **spellcasting focus**.

---

### Combat Statistics
* **Base Weapon:** Quarterstaff (1d6 bludgeoning, versatile 1d8 bludgeoning).
* **Magic Bonus:** You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this weapon, and a **+1 bonus to your Bard spell save DC**.

---

### Special Properties

#### 1. Percussion Flourish (Bardic Resonance)
Whenever you expend a use of **Bardic Inspiration** (including for features like Blade Flourishes or Mantle of Inspiration), a spectral drizzle erupts in a 30-foot radius around you:
* **Soothing Shower:** You or one ally within 30 feet gains temporary hit points equal to the number rolled on your inspiration die.
* **Extinguish:** Nonmagical flames within 15 feet of you are immediately snuffed out.

#### 2. Downpour (5 Charges)
The staff holds **5 charges** and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the staff, you can expend charges to cast one of the following spells (using your Bard spell save DC):
* **Fog Cloud** (1 charge) - A thick, swirling bank of storm vapor fills a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 120 feet (Concentration, up to 1 hour).
* **Thunderwave** (1 charge) - A wave of thunderous force sweeps out from you in a 15-foot cube (2d8 thunder damage, 10-foot push and knocked prone on a failed Constitution save).
* **Create or Destroy Water** (1 charge) - You create up to 10 gallons of clean water, or make rain fall in a 30-foot cube, dousing open flames.
* **Tidal Wave** (3 charges) - A crushing wave of water 30 feet long, 10 feet wide, and 10 feet tall slams into creatures (4d8 bludgeoning damage and knocked prone on a failed Dexterity save).

#### 3. Heavy Drop (Impact)
Once on each of your turns when you hit a target with a melee attack using this staff, you can make the blow land with the crushing weight of pressurized water. 

The attack deals an extra **1d6 cold or thunder damage** (your choice), and if the target is Large or smaller, its movement speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

---

### Appearance & Lore
* **Visual:** Dark, weather-beaten wood capped with oxidized bronze ferrules. Running water runes are carved along the shaft, glowing faint sea-green whenever a spell is channeled through it.
* **Sensory:** Swung slowly, it sounds like steady autumn rain on a tin roof. Swung hard in battle, the internal beads roar like a flash flood.
