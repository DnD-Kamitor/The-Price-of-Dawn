<summary>S1C1 — Lowmark Junction (Combat)</summary>

### Where
Lowmark Junction. A cramped four-way street intersection in the lower residential district. ~50×50 ft. Worn cobblestones, iron lantern posts at corners, second-storey washing lines overhead. The permanent amber twilight is thickest here — lamplight pools low and doesn't reach upper storeys.

### Why They're Here
Players are walking to the Civic Archive to begin their investigation. Restorer zealots are already here — not to kill, to warn. They've been tipped that investigators are moving on the archive and have positioned to intercept.

### Environment
- **Sight:** A food distribution queue running east from the south lane (10–15 civilians bundled close). A heavy cart loaded with cloth-wrapped parcels at centre-south. Market crates stacked 3–4 high against the east wall. Zealots positioned to block all four exits.
- **Sound:** The low murmur of the queue, a child asking when the cart opens, the click of boot heels on cobbles as zealots take position.
- **Smell:** Woodsmoke from the nearest hearth, damp wool, the faint sourness of too many people in a narrow space.
- **Touch:** The cold is deep here — no wind, just still trapped air. The cobbles are slick where the queue has worn them.
- **Taste:** Coal-smoke in the back of the throat.

**Key features:** Fireplace on NE lantern post (note-burn threat), the distribution cart (blocking cover), the east alley mouth (Edoran hiding at 30 ft).

### NPCs
| NPC | Who | Stats |
|---|---|---|
| **Restorer Enforcer** | Former city guard (resigned yr42 grey sickness cuts). Field commander. Delivers sealed note. Not trying to kill — trying to stop. | Veteran (MM p.350) |
| **Restorer Zealots ×4** | True believers, not thugs. Motivated by grief and theology. Each positioned at an exit. | Guard (MM p.347) |
| **Brother Edoran** | Does not engage. Watches from 30 ft inside east alley. Shadows, motionless. DC 15 Perception to spot. Revealed in Session 2+. | — |

**The sealed note** (Enforcer carries it): *"The willing heart gives freely what the bound heart cannot."* Signed: Edoran.

<summary>S1C2 — Civic Archive Door (Puzzle)</summary>

### Where
The Civic Archive, main entrance. A large stone institutional building in the Spire Quarter. The restricted stacks door is set into the north wall of the main reading room — heavy oak, iron-bound, a riddle carved in Archmagister Corven's private notation above the frame.

### Why They're Here
Players need documents about the Ritual of Eternal Dawn. The public stacks contain nothing useful. Theron Waide (Master Archivist) meets them here and leads them to the restricted door — he has been waiting for someone to come looking. He will not open the door for them. He will not say the answer. He is waiting for someone else to carry the knowledge.

### Environment
- **Sight:** The main reading room: long oak tables, oil lamps, the smell of a building that processes information. The restricted door is darker than the wood around it — old, used rarely. The riddle is carved in neat formal notation above the lintel.
- **Sound:** Quiet. A building that asks for quiet. Distant sound of papers being moved somewhere above.
- **Smell:** Old paper, leather bindings, lamp oil, and very faintly — something electrical from behind the door (the star-wheel mechanism).
- **Touch:** The door is cool and solid. The carved letters are deep-cut, precise. Someone took care with them.
- **Taste:** Nothing. Archive air, dry.

**The riddle** (carved above the door): Corven's private notation. Answer: **CONSENT**. Theron knows. He will not say it. He has been waiting eleven years for someone to read it.

### NPCs
| NPC | Who |
|---|---|
| **Theron Waide** | Master Archivist, 50s, angular, ink-stained. Has known the truth for 11 years and said nothing — guilt, not malice. Has been slowly destroying his own notes. Mild, precise manner. Will not lie but will not volunteer. Want: to give the knowledge to someone else without being the one who gives it. Fear: being the person who condemned 10 people. Lie: that he doesn't know the answer to the riddle. |

<summary>S1C3 — The Restricted Stacks (Puzzle)</summary>

### Where
The restricted stacks — behind the door from S1C2. A long narrow room of floor-to-ceiling shelves, lit by amber lanterns on wall brackets. At the far end: a central column with three brass rings (the star-wheel mechanism). On the east wall: a large star chart. Shelf 4-17-3 is the target.

### Why They're Here
The restricted stacks hold Corven's original ritual documentation. The star-wheel is the lock on the relevant shelf. Players need to decode the night-sky configuration to retrieve Fragment 1 of the Notation Key and Corven's public summary.

### Environment
- **Sight:** Tall shelves in near-darkness except for the lanterns. The star-wheel at the far end: three concentric brass rings on a central column, each with constellation engravings. The east wall's star chart, hand-drawn, annotated in two different inks — one old (Corven), one newer (Theron).
- **Sound:** Silence and the occasional creak of the building settling. The star-wheel's rings move smoothly when turned — a soft brass-on-brass whisper.
- **Smell:** Intensified old paper and dust. No airflow back here. The lantern smoke sits in the air.
- **Touch:** The rings are polished smooth from use. The constellation engravings are sharp-edged, precise. The brass is slightly warm — the mechanism has trace resonance.
- **Taste:** Dust. The back of the throat dries out.

**Star-wheel solution:** Set rings to **Vael's Crown / Shepherd's Eye / Trailing Light** (Dawnmark constellation, west-to-east rise order). Star chart on east wall contains the solution in plain language. Shelf 4-17-3 opens.

### NPCs
| NPC | Who |
|---|---|
| **Theron Waide** | Present but does not enter. Remains near the door. He has not been into this room in three years. He is not ready to come in again. If players call to him for help, he answers from the doorway. |

**What's on shelf 4-17-3:**
- Corven's public summary (*"distributed sympathetic anchors"* and *"living conduit mechanism"* without definitions)
- Fragment 1 of Corven's Notation Key (cipher text + two plain-language glosses, including: *"The weight borne willingly is not the same weight as the weight imposed. This is not a philosophical observation. It is an engineering specification."*)

### Where
Same reading room, post-combat. Overturned chairs, the fireplace still hot. Theron has emerged from the alcove. He is at the central reading table. He has unlocked his personal desk drawer and placed three small parchment slips on the table in front of the players.

### Why They're Here
Theron has been carrying these slips for eleven years. He encoded his own knowledge so he could deny it existed. He left it solvable. He is waiting for someone to decode it, because he cannot give the information directly — he needs them to take it.

### Environment
- **Sight:** The settling aftermath of the combat. The slips on the table: small parchments, folded so many times the creases are deep as scars. Theron's hands as he places them — slightly unsteady, not visibly trembling.
- **Sound:** Quiet returning. The Archive settling. Distant building creaks.
- **Smell:** Old paper, the char of the fireplace still present. The slips themselves: slightly dusty, dry — they have been in a locked drawer.
- **Touch:** The parchment is brittle at fold lines. One slip feels slightly different — it was rewritten once, the original destroyed.

<summary>S2C3 — Theron's Private Study (Puzzle)</summary>

### Where
Upper floor of the Civic Archive. Theron's private study — smaller than the reading room, cluttered. Books stacked sideways on top of rows. Star charts on the west wall with annotations in red ink. On the shelf behind his desk: a sealed wooden box the size of a large book. On its lid: three circular brass indentations.

### Why They're Here
Theron leads players here after the slips are decoded. The box has been sitting on this shelf for eleven years. It contains Corven's original handwritten ritual redirection notes — physical proof of what happened the night the Ritual of Eternal Dawn "redirected." Theron has never opened it. He knows the answer. He will not say it to the box himself.

### Environment
- **Sight:** Cramped study, desk covered in papers. Star charts on west wall annotated in two ink colours. The box on the shelf: wooden, smooth, no visible keyhole. Three brass indentations on the lid, spaced evenly — left, centre, right. Each slightly warm to the touch.
- **Sound:** Quieter than the main floor. One window, closed. The building's sounds are muffled up here. When a correct name is spoken into an indentation: a resonant hum, like a crystal glass struck gently.
- **Smell:** Years of paper and old tea in a closed room.
- **Touch:** The brass indentations have trace warmth — the mechanism has residual resonance even when sealed.
- **Taste:** Dry air. Room rarely opened.

**The puzzle:** Speak the three witness names from Corven's colophon, left to right: **CORVEN → SALI → WREN**. Names found in document bundle colophons from S2C1. Wrong name: discordant tone, 30-second reset. Partial state holds — two correct names stay active if third is wrong.

**Inside the box:** Corven's ritual redirection notes. Key passage: *"The anchors are not lost. The light did not die. It redistributed into ten living vessels born at the moment of inversion. The ritual is incomplete, not failed. The completion requires their release."* + Fragment 2 of Notation Key.

### NPCs
| NPC | Who |
|---|---|
| **Theron Waide** | Leads them here. Stands at the doorway, does not approach the box. Has never pressed the indentations himself. Knows all three names. Will not volunteer them. If asked directly: goes very still, then says "Yes. In that order. Left to right." Looks away. |
